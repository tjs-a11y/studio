Water projects need a safety check—not just a shopping list

Before buying a water-related DIY guide, establish what problem you are solving and what water-quality standard the finished system must meet. A design or filter claim does not prove that water is safe to drink.

  • Confirm whether the offer is instructions only or includes equipment.
  • Identify the contaminants that must be tested and the independent lab or authority that can verify results.
  • Check local plumbing, electrical, building and drinking-water requirements; use qualified installation where required.
