    Know this before you call
    
    
    On a credit card, the Fair Credit Billing Act generally gives you 60 days from the statement that shows the error to dispute it in writing; your liability for unauthorized use is capped at $50, and the issuer must acknowledge within 30 days and resolve within two billing cycles (up to 90 days). On a debit card, Regulation E ties your liability to speed: report within 2 business days to cap it at $50, within 60 days to cap it at $500 — so act fast. This is general information, not legal advice.
