Safety first

Tick every box to continue. These tests involve standing up and balancing.

The one-foot balance stage is optional. Only try it with the helper right beside the person.

How should we count?

Use the camera

Prop the phone 2–3 m (7–10 ft) away at hip height, from the side, so the chair and the whole body are in view. Good light and fitted clothes help.

Count by hand

No camera. Tap a button for each stand and use the on-screen stopwatch.

Test 1 · 30-second chair stand

Sit in the middle of the chair, feet flat, arms crossed over the chest. On "Go", stand all the way up and sit all the way down, as many times as possible in 30 seconds.

Test 2 · 4-stage balance

Hold each position for 10 seconds without moving the feet or holding on. Arms can move to help balance. No cane or walker, and keep the eyes open. The helper stands close. Stop at the first one that can't be held.

    Test 3 · Timed Up and Go

    Put a mark 3 m (10 ft) from the chair: tape, a shoe, or 4 big floor tiles. On "Go", stand up, walk to the mark at a normal pace, turn, walk back and sit down. Normal walking aid is fine.

    What this means

    Below average on any test? The CDC says that indicates a higher risk of falls. Share this page with a doctor or physical therapist. Strength and balance exercise lowers the rate of falls in older people.
