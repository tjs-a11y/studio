How steady are you on your feet?

Try the CDC's three fall-risk screening tests at home, with a phone and a helper.

  1. 30-second chair standHow many times can you stand up from a chair, arms crossed, in 30 seconds?
  2. 4-stage balance testHold four standing positions for 10 seconds each, each one harder than the last.
  3. Timed Up and GoStand up, walk 10 feet (3 meters), turn, walk back and sit down, at your normal pace.

Safety first: always have a second adult stand close by during every test. Use a sturdy chair without arms, pushed against a wall. Stop at once if there is dizziness, pain or unsteadiness.

Footsure compares each result with the cutoffs printed on the CDC's 2017 STEADI assessment forms. It is a general wellness check, not a medical diagnosis and not a medical device. A result below the CDC cutoff is a reason to talk to a doctor or physical therapist. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the CDC. Footsure is made by Quickwell.
