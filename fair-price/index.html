Don't approve a quote you cannot explain line by line.

A quote can be high because the scope is unclear, a fee is bundled, or an optional service is presented as required. Review what is included before you agree—not just the final total.

  • Paste the estimate and see structural red flags or missing details.
  • Ask what each line covers, what is optional and what could change the price.
  • Get another written quote when you need a real market comparison.
