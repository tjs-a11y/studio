Bill Radar

Read your power bill and see what's coming for it: the date your fixed rate rolls off, a 12-month forecast, and the moves to make before it hits. Every number is an estimate you control — nothing is uploaded.

Not sure of the rate? Divide last month's total $ by the kWh used (both on the bill), then move the decimal: $150 ÷ 1000 kWh = $0.15 = 15¢.

Assumptions (estimates — adjust them)

These are scenario assumptions, not utility data or predictions. Zero means no change; a positive number models an increase and a negative number models a decrease. What happens when a plan ends depends on your contract, provider and local rules; check the renewal notice for the actual next rate.

What to do before your rate rolls off

  • Find your end date. It's on your electricity plan's "Electricity Facts Label" or contract. Put it above to see the month it lands.
  • Check the renewal terms. Depending on your contract and market, the provider may offer a renewal, move you to a variable product or require you to choose a new plan. Compare the actual next rate and fees; do not assume it will be higher.
  • Compare apples to apples at your usage level (e.g. ~1000 kWh) — the headline rate often hides fees that only show up at certain usage.
  • In a deregulated state (Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Illinois and others) you can switch supplier without changing wires or losing power.
