About the science

These games are for fun. Each one practises a skill: Echo short-term memory, Swift quick attention, Linkr planning and arithmetic. You will get better at each game as you play it, and your stats show that progress.

Research has not shown that brain games raise general intelligence, prevent memory loss or decline, or make you better at everyday tasks, and we don't claim they do. If you are worried about your memory, talk to a doctor.
