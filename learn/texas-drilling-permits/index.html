Texas drilling permits by RRC district

The Railroad Commission of Texas (RRC) runs oil and gas regulation through 13 districts. Each page below lists a district's counties, its office, the major formations the RRC associates with it, recent permit numbers, and how to follow new permits.

Why districts matter to mineral owners

Every Texas drilling permit (Form W-1) carries a district. District offices handle field operations, inspections and local questions, and RRC statistics are reported by district. If you own minerals or land in a county, knowing its district tells you which office to call and how busy the area is.

The RRC's production filing rules list 13 district codes: 01 to 10, plus 6E, 7B, 7C and 8A (there is no 07 or 8B in use) (RRC Form PR filing requirements). Twelve of them divide up the state's 254 counties; 6E is used for the East Texas Field and has no counties of its own in the RRC's county table.
