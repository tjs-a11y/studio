Understanding your electricity bill and rate

A fixed electricity rate feels settled until it ends. These pages explain what a plan end date can mean, how to read your own bill, and what the latest EIA national average can — and cannot — tell you.

Why the end of a plan matters more than the plan

Most people choose an electricity plan once and forget it. The problem is that fixed-rate plans have an end date. Depending on the market and contract, a provider may move you to a month-to-month variable plan, where the price can change. Check your own plan documents rather than assuming every provider uses the same roll-off. These pages explain the mechanics from public sources, and the free Bill Radar tool turns your own rate and usage into a dated forecast so the roll-off never surprises you.

Nothing here is a specific company's rate or a prediction of your bill. Every page lists the source and the date it was checked, and the tool works only from numbers you type in, on your device.
