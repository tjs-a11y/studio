How do I get a file from my bank?
  1. Sign in to your bank or card website (the app may not offer it).
  2. Open the account, then look for "Download", "Export" or "Statements".
  3. Choose CSV (or a PDF statement). Pick the last 12 months if you can: at least 3 months is needed to spot monthly charges.
  4. Add each account and card you use. Then drop the files here.

Privacy

  • Your files are read by this page, in your browser. They are never uploaded, logged or stored on a server.
  • We never ask for bank logins, passwords or card numbers. Please never type them here.
  • Saver keeps your marks and savings history in this browser only (local storage). Clear them any time below.
  • The page sends one anonymous count when someone checks a real file (no names, amounts or file contents), and talks to our payment server only if you buy. PDF reading loads pdf.js from cdnjs, only when you choose a PDF.
