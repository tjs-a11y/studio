Proof

Moving out, picking up a rental car, or opening a box that arrived broken? Add a photo and Proof records your device-clock time plus a SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact file. Later, compare another copy to see whether its bytes changed. It does not verify who took the photo, when it was first captured, or whether it was edited before recording. The photo never leaves your phone.
