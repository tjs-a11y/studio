FTC Sues Toast Over Hidden Fees, Probes Personalized Pricing at Nine Firms

Regulators sued Toast over hidden fees and cancellation tactics and ordered airlines, ride-hailing and delivery firms to explain data-based pricing.

Federal and state regulators are cracking down on hidden fees and hard-to-cancel subscriptions. The FTC is also asking whether your personal data shapes the prices you're charged.

FTC and States Sue Toast

  • The FTC, California local officials and attorneys general from seven states sued Toast Inc., a restaurant software and fintech company.
  • The complaint alleges hidden and unauthorized fees, a pattern worth watching on any bill you receive.
  • Regulators also accuse Toast of deceptive subscription and cancellation practices, a growing enforcement focus.
  • The agencies say Toast misled customers about costs and its promised "24/7/365" customer support.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC and States Take Action Against Toast Inc. over Hidden and Unauthorized Fees, Deceptive Subscription and Cancellation Practices and Other Unlawful Conduct

Is Your Data Setting Your Price?

  • The FTC ordered nine major airlines, ride-sharing companies and food-delivery firms to hand over detailed information.
  • Regulators want to learn how these companies use personal data to set prices, which could mean different shoppers pay different amounts.
  • The inquiry also covers how personal data shapes compensation, such as what drivers and couriers are paid.
  • This is a study, not an enforcement action, so no company has been accused of wrongdoing yet.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Launches Personalized Pricing and Compensation Inquiry
