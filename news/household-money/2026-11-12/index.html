Car insurance costs climb nearly 38% for drivers with poor credit

Auto premiums jumped for drivers with weak credit, plus a new balance transfer card and a store card that requires a membership.

Weak credit, steeper car insurance

  • Premiums rose nearly 38% over three years for drivers with poor credit, according to a 2026 auto insurance rates report.
  • A credit score can affect more than loan rates; it can also raise a recurring household bill.
  • Drivers whose credit has slipped may face higher renewal quotes than they expect.

Source: NerdWallet: Car Insurance Soars for Drivers With Poor Credit; Here’s What to Do

New balance transfer card arrives

  • Capital One has launched a new VentureOne card built for balance transfers.
  • It is described as one of the few balance transfer cards that stays useful after the no-interest promo period ends.
  • For anyone carrying card debt, the terms after the promo window matter as much as the interest-free period itself.

Source: NerdWallet: Capital One Launches New VentureOne Balance Transfer Card

A store card with a membership catch

  • The Fabletics credit card is open only to people who are Fabletics VIP members.
  • That requirement adds a hurdle and ties the card to an ongoing retailer relationship.
  • The membership decision comes before the card decision, which narrows who can realistically apply.

Source: NerdWallet: 5 Things to Know About the Fabletics Credit Card
