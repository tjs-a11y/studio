American Airlines Card Fee Rises to $150 as Holiday Gift Budgets Climb

A popular airline card adds $51 to its annual fee, and shoppers expect to spend nearly $200 more on gifts this year.

If you carry the Citi / AAdvantage Platinum Select card, its annual fee is rising to $150. Check whether the new perks cover the extra cost before it renews.

Airline Card Fee Climbs $51

  • The Citi / AAdvantage Platinum Select World Elite Mastercard's annual fee is rising to $150, so cardholders will pay more each year.
  • The increase is $51, a cost that shows up on your statement whether or not you fly.
  • A new $100 annual in-flight credit is among the added perks, but it only helps if you'll actually use it.
  • Compare the higher fee with what you'd realistically use before renewing, or consider a no-fee card.

Source: NerdWallet: American Airlines Revamps Platinum Select Credit Card, Increases Annual Fee to $150

Holiday Gift Budgets Swell

  • Shoppers plan to spend nearly $200 more on gifts, on average, than last year, according to a new survey.
  • Tariff-related price increases may be part of the reason, so the same gift list could cost more.
  • Setting a firm gift budget now can help keep holiday spending from turning into card debt.
