CFPB Opens New Push on Debanking by Large Banks

The CFPB is acting on debanking cases uncovered by the OCC, and new application data shows secured card approvals by credit score band.

The CFPB has announced a new initiative on debanking at large banks, building on findings from the OCC, the national bank regulator.

CFPB Takes Aim at Debanking

  • The CFPB announced a new initiative on October 14 targeting debanking by large banks.
  • The effort builds on debanking cases the OCC uncovered, so it focuses on the biggest institutions.
  • Debanking means closing or refusing accounts; the CFPB's focus is on decisions tied to constitutionally protected rights.
  • The bureau calls this the next step in its work against debanking of businesses and individuals, signaling more action may follow.

Source: CFPB Newsroom: CFPB Announces New Initiative to Address OCC-Uncovered Debanking by Large Banks

Secured Card Approvals by Credit Score

  • New application data breaks down secured credit card approval rates by credit score band.
  • Secured cards require a deposit and are a common tool for building or rebuilding credit.
  • Results come from real applications, giving a practical view of how scores affect approval odds.

Source: NerdWallet: What Credit Score Do You Need for a Secured Credit Card? (Based on Real Applications)
