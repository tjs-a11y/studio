Thursday, November 5, 2026 · Household Money Brief

Payment Processor Pays $600,000 Over Hidden Fees; New Details in Amazon Ads Case

Regulators target undisclosed fees and auto-renewing contracts, while unredacted filings detail alleged price inflation in Amazon's ad auctions.

A payment processor and its leasing affiliate will pay $600,000 to settle claims they hid fees and auto-renewing contracts from small businesses.

Hidden Fees Cost Processor $600,000

  • $600,000 settlement: Riverside Payments, Cascade Equipment Leasing and their operators resolve FTC and Washington state allegations.
  • Regulators allege the firms promised small businesses savings on card processing but didn't disclose fees.
  • Auto-renewing contracts were also allegedly concealed, a trap that also catches consumers in service agreements.
  • Takeaway for anyone signing a contract: check fee schedules and cancellation terms before agreeing.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC, Washington Take Action Against Payment Processor for Deceiving Small Businesses About Costs and Cancellation Terms

Amazon Ad Case Gets More Detail

  • 22 states and the FTC filed an updated complaint with previously redacted details against Amazon.
  • Regulators allege Amazon secretly manipulated its search ad auctions to inflate prices.
  • The filing claims auction results were adjusted to hit corporate financial targets.
  • These are allegations in an active case, not findings, so watch how the court responds.
