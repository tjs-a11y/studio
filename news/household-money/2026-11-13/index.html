FTC Fights Vet Supply Megadeal

  • The FTC sued to block Covetrus from buying rival MWI Veterinary Supply for $3.5 billion.
  • Regulators say the pair are the two biggest distributors of veterinary products and services.
  • The agency argues the deal would push up costs for clinics, which could pass higher prices on to pet owners.
  • Covetrus owners Clayton, Dubilier & Rice and TPG are also named in the suit.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues to Block Veterinary Supplies Distributor Covetrus’s $3.5 Billion Acquisition of Rival MWI

Weak Credit, Pricier Car Insurance

  • Drivers with poor credit saw auto premiums rise nearly 38% over the past three years, a 2026 rates report found.
  • Your credit standing can directly affect what you pay to insure a car, not just borrowing costs.
  • Households with damaged credit face a growing gap on a bill most drivers cannot skip.

Source: NerdWallet: Car Insurance Soars for Drivers With Poor Credit; Here’s What to Do
