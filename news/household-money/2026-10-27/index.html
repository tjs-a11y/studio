Popular Airline Card Raises Annual Fee to $150 as Holiday Budgets Swell

An American Airlines co-branded card adds $51 to its yearly fee, and shoppers expect to spend nearly $200 more on gifts.

The Citi / AAdvantage Platinum Select card's annual fee is going up $51, to $150. Cardholders should check whether the new perks offset the higher charge.

Airline Card Fee Rises to $150

  • The Citi / AAdvantage Platinum Select World Elite Mastercard is raising its annual fee by $51.
  • The new yearly fee is $150, a cost cardholders pay whether or not they fly.
  • New perks include a $100 in-flight credit each year, worth it only if you actually buy onboard.
  • Compare the added benefits to your real spending before your renewal date hits.

Holiday Gift Budgets Climb

  • Shoppers expect to spend nearly $200 more on gifts, on average, than last year, a NerdWallet survey found.
  • Tariff-related price increases may be part of the reason, so the same gifts could simply cost more.
  • Setting a firm gift budget now can help avoid card balances that linger into the new year.

Source: NerdWallet: 2025 Holiday Spending Report

Bigger Sign-Up Bonuses on Atmos Cards

  • Two Atmos Rewards Visa cards raised their welcome offers to as much as 100,000 points.
  • Large bonuses usually require meeting a spending threshold, which can push households to overspend.
  • Weigh any annual fee against the bonus before applying for a new card.

Source: NerdWallet: Atmos Credit Cards Increase Welcome Offers Up to 100,000 Points
