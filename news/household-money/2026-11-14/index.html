FTC Targets Vet Supply Merger

  • The FTC filed suit to stop Covetrus from acquiring MWI Veterinary Supply for $3.5 billion.
  • The two firms are the biggest distributors of veterinary products and services, according to the FTC.
  • Regulators allege the combination would raise costs for vet clinics, which could reach pet owners' bills.
  • Covetrus owners Clayton, Dubilier & Rice and TPG are also named in the suit.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues to Block Veterinary Supplies Distributor Covetrus’s $3.5 Billion Acquisition of Rival MWI
