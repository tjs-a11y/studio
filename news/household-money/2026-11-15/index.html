FTC Sues to Stop Vet Supply Merger It Says Could Raise Pet Care Costs

Regulators challenge Covetrus's $3.5 billion bid for rival MWI, and two store and brokerage credit cards get a closer look.

The FTC has sued to block Covetrus from buying its closest rival, MWI, for $3.5 billion. The agency warns the deal could push up prices for vet clinics and pet owners.

FTC Targets Vet Supply Merger

  • $3.5 billion: the price of Covetrus's planned purchase of MWI Veterinary Supply, which the FTC has sued to block.
  • The FTC says the deal would join the two largest distributors of veterinary products and services.
  • Regulators allege the merger would lift costs for clinics, which pet owners could see in their vet bills.
  • Covetrus owners Clayton, Dubilier & Rice and TPG are also named in the suit.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues to Block Veterinary Supplies Distributor Covetrus’s $3.5 Billion Acquisition of Rival MWI

Store and Brokerage Cards Explained

  • The Albertsons forU American Express Card works at any store in the Albertsons family.
  • Each Albertsons-family store also offers its own dedicated card, so shoppers may face several similar choices.
  • The Ameriprise credit card's perks mainly benefit people who already hold Ameriprise accounts.
  • For anyone without an Ameriprise account, the card offers little value, a review finds.

Source: NerdWallet: 5 Things to Know About the Albertsons Credit Cards · NerdWallet: 5 Things to Know About the Ameriprise Credit Card
