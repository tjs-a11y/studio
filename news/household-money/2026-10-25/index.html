Shifting bill due dates can smooth cash flow, Atmos cards raise welcome offers, and the FTC sues to block a playground-equipment merger.

If your bills land before your paycheck, asking creditors to move your due dates is a simple way to ease the squeeze.

Line Up Due Dates With Payday

  • You can request a change to a bill's due date, which helps when payments hit before your paycheck arrives.
  • Matching due dates to your pay schedule can make your monthly cash flow easier to manage.

Source: NerdWallet: Asked on Reddit: My Bills Are Due Before Payday — What Do I Do?

Atmos Cards Boost Sign-Up Bonuses

  • Two Atmos Rewards credit cards, the Ascent and the Summit, raised their welcome offers to as much as 100,000 points.
  • A bigger bonus only pays off if you can meet spending requirements without carrying a balance or paying interest.

Source: NerdWallet: Atmos Credit Cards Increase Welcome Offers Up to 100,000 Points

FTC Moves to Block Playground Merger

  • On 2026-10-19, the FTC sued to stop PlayPower, owned by Platinum Equity, from buying rival BCI Burke.
  • The agency says combining two of the largest commercial playground makers would weaken competition on price and quality.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues to Block PlayPower’s Acquisition of Rival Playground Manufacturer Burke
