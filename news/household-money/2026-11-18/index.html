Family Dollar's $63 Million Price Settlement

  • The FTC and 17 states reached a $63 million settlement with Family Dollar Stores.
  • Regulators allege the chain routinely rang up prices above those on shelves, price tags and ads.
  • The alleged overcharges cost consumers millions of dollars, which makes checking receipts against shelf prices worthwhile.
  • Officials frame the deal as a win for price transparency at the checkout.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC and 17 States Secure $63 Million Settlement with Family Dollar Stores in Price Transparency Win for Consumers

FTC Moves to Block Vet Supply Merger

  • The FTC sued to stop Covetrus from buying rival MWI Veterinary Supply in a $3.5 billion deal.
  • The agency says the two are the biggest distributors of veterinary products and services.
  • The FTC argues a merger would push up costs for clinics and, ultimately, for pet owners.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues to Block Veterinary Supplies Distributor Covetrus’s $3.5 Billion Acquisition of Rival MWI
