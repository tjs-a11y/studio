Bills Due Before Payday

  • Requesting a new due date from billers can help align payments with when your paycheck arrives.
  • Better-timed due dates can make monthly cash flow easier to manage and reduce last-minute scrambles.

Source: NerdWallet: Asked on Reddit: My Bills Are Due Before Payday — What Do I Do?

FTC Sues to Block Playground Merger

  • The FTC sued to stop PlayPower, owned by Platinum Equity, from acquiring rival playground maker BCI Burke.
  • The agency says the two are among the largest makers of commercial outdoor playground equipment.
  • Regulators argue the deal would weaken price and quality competition in an already concentrated market.
