Weak credit, pricier car insurance

  • Premiums for drivers with poor credit are up nearly 38% over the past three years, according to NerdWallet's 2026 rates report.
  • Many insurers factor credit into pricing, so a low score can quietly raise your renewal bill.
  • Check your renewal notice against last year's to spot how much your premium has moved.

Source: NerdWallet: Car Insurance Soars for Drivers With Poor Credit; Here’s What to Do

FTC unseals more in Amazon case

  • The FTC and 22 states filed an updated complaint against Amazon with previously redacted details.
  • Regulators allege Amazon secretly manipulated its search-ad auctions to inflate prices and hit financial targets.
  • The claims are allegations for now; the case shows regulators are still pursuing hidden pricing tactics.
