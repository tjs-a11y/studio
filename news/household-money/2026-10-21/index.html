Line Up Due Dates With Payday

  • Billers can be asked to move your due date, a simple fix when payments hit before paychecks.
  • Matching due dates to payday can make monthly cash flow easier to manage.
  • This step targets timing, not the amount owed, so it helps most when money arrives a bit too late.

Source: NerdWallet: Asked on Reddit: My Bills Are Due Before Payday — What Do I Do?

FTC Challenges Playground Equipment Merger

  • The FTC filed suit to stop PlayPower, owned by Platinum Equity, from acquiring rival BCI Burke.
  • The agency says the two firms are among the largest makers of commercial outdoor playground equipment.
  • Regulators argue the merger would weaken competition on price and quality in an already concentrated market.
