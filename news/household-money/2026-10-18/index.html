CFPB Opens New Push on Debanking by Large Banks; Secured Card Approval Data

The CFPB is acting on debanking findings from the OCC, and new application data shows how credit scores affect secured card approvals.

The CFPB has launched a new initiative against debanking by large banks, building on findings from the OCC.

CFPB takes aim at debanking

  • The CFPB announced a new initiative on 2026-10-14 targeting debanking by large banks.
  • It builds on debanking cases uncovered by the OCC, the federal regulator of national banks.
  • The bureau frames it as protecting people and businesses cut off over constitutionally protected rights.
  • If a bank has closed your account without clear reason, keep your letters and records.

Source: CFPB Newsroom: CFPB Announces New Initiative to Address OCC-Uncovered Debanking by Large Banks

Secured card approvals by credit score

  • New application data breaks down secured credit card approval rates by credit score band.
  • Secured cards require a deposit and are often a first step for rebuilding damaged credit.
  • Checking where your score falls can help you avoid applications likely to be denied.

Source: NerdWallet: What Credit Score Do You Need for a Secured Credit Card? (Based on Real Applications)
