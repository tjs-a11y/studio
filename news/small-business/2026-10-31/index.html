IRS Wants Action Before Year-End

  • The IRS is recommending five steps now, so start year-end prep early rather than waiting for filing season.
  • The agency frames these steps as a way to prevent unwelcome surprises at April filing time.
  • The IRS Advisory Council has scheduled a public meeting, a chance to watch agency priorities take shape.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Do These 5 Things Before the End of 2026, IRS Urges · Federal Register: IRS: Notice: Internal Revenue Service Advisory Council; Public Meeting

Chicago Head Tax Back on Table

  • Mayor Brandon Johnson is again proposing a per-employee corporate tax in his latest city budget plan.
  • Gov. JB Pritzker restated his opposition on Thursday, signaling continued state-level resistance.
  • Chicago employers should track the budget debate, since a head tax would raise payroll-linked costs.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Illinois Gov. JB Pritzker Again Takes Dim View of Chicago Mayor’s Head Tax Plan

Wealth Tax Fights in Two States

  • Colorado's Supreme Court on Friday rejected a bid to void the vote on Amendment 87, a tax hike on top earners.
  • In California, a billionaire-backed group spent more than $50 million in one week opposing Proposition 40's wealth tax.
  • High-income owners in both states should watch outcomes that could reshape their personal tax planning.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Colorado Supreme Court Rejects Lawsuit Seeking to Stop Income Tax Measure · CPA Practice Advisor: Billionaires Flood Final Stretch with $50M to Sink Prop. 40, California’s Wealth Tax

Film Credit Push in Lame Duck

  • A bipartisan coalition is pressing to pass a federal film tax credit before the 119th Congress ends.
  • Production-related small businesses could benefit if lawmakers act in the remaining weeks.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Backers of Federal Film Tax Credit Seek Action in Lame-Duck Session
