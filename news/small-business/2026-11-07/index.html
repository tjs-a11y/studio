IRS Posts 2027 Inflation Adjustments

  • Nearly 70 tax provisions got inflation updates for tax year 2027, the IRS says.
  • Income tax brackets and the standard deduction are among the items adjusted.
  • Owners and freelancers should use the new figures when planning 2027 estimated payments and payroll withholding.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: IRS Adjusts Tax Brackets, Standard Deduction for Tax Year 2027

Election Day Rewrites State Tax Rules

  • Florida voters approved Amendment 3, a property tax cut that forces local governments to decide which services to trim.
  • Colorado's Amendment 87 backers claim victory on a measure raising income taxes on high earners.
  • Washington's high-earner income tax stands after Initiative 645 backers conceded defeat.
  • Business owners in these states should watch for local fee, levy and service changes as rollouts begin.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: 5 Questions After Florida Voters Approved Property Tax Ballot Amendment · CPA Practice Advisor: ‘We’re Going to Be Underwater’: What Florida’s Property Tax Cut Means for Flooding · CPA Practice Advisor: Supporters of Colorado’s Amendment 87 Declare Victory for Major Rewrite of State’s Tax Code · CPA Practice Advisor: Washington ‘Millionaires Tax’ is Upheld as Initiative 645 Fails

Over 6,500 Opportunity Zones Named

  • Treasury and the IRS designated more than 6,500 communities as opportunity zones.
  • The designations span all 50 states, the District of Columbia and five U.S. territories.
  • Owners weighing new locations or investments should check whether their area made the list.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Treasury, IRS Designate Over 6,500 Communities as Opportunity Zones

AICPA Seeks Penalty Relief for Trusts

  • The AICPA asked the IRS to waive 2026 estimated tax penalties for certain estates and trusts.
  • Open questions remain on how the provision fits with rules specific to estates and trusts.
  • Bookkeepers handling fiduciary returns should hold off on final penalty calculations until the IRS responds.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: AICPA Calls on IRS to Waive 2026 Estimated Tax Penalties for Certain Estates and Trusts
