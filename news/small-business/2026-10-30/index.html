Dyed Diesel Penalties on Hold

  • Federal tax penalties are suspended through the rest of this year when dyed diesel is sold for or used on U.S. highways.
  • The IRS posted new FAQs this week explaining how the relief works.
  • Fleet, construction and farm operators should review the FAQs to confirm their fuel use qualifies.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: IRS Issues FAQs on Tax Relief for Dyed Diesel Fuel Highway Use

IRS Flags Five Year-End Moves

  • The IRS is urging taxpayers to complete five tasks before the end of 2026.
  • Acting now aims to smooth the coming filing season and head off surprises in April.
  • The IRS Advisory Council will also hold a public meeting, a chance to track agency priorities.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Do These 5 Things Before the End of 2026, IRS Urges · Federal Register: IRS: Notice: Internal Revenue Service Advisory Council; Public Meeting

Chicago Head Tax Faces Pushback

  • Mayor Brandon Johnson has again proposed a per-employee corporate tax in his latest city budget pitch.
  • Gov. JB Pritzker repeated his opposition on Thursday.
  • Chicago employers should watch the budget talks, since a per-head levy would add a direct cost to hiring.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Illinois Gov. JB Pritzker Again Takes Dim View of Chicago Mayor’s Head Tax Plan

Big Money Targets California Wealth Tax

  • A billionaire-backed group spent more than $50 million over the past week to defeat Proposition 40.
  • Prop. 40 is a California wealth tax heading into the final stretch before the vote.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Billionaires Flood Final Stretch with $50M to Sink Prop. 40, California’s Wealth Tax
