2027 tax numbers are out

  • The IRS updated nearly 70 tax provisions for tax year 2027 to account for inflation.
  • Brackets and the standard deduction both shift, so owners should revisit withholding and estimated payment plans.
  • Bookkeepers can start building the 2027 figures into client planning now.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: IRS Adjusts Tax Brackets, Standard Deduction for Tax Year 2027

Preparers: renew your PTIN

  • Current preparer tax identification numbers expire on Dec. 31, 2026.
  • Anyone paid to prepare returns needs a valid PTIN, and it must be renewed every year.
  • Renewing early avoids a gap heading into filing season.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Attention Tax Pros: It’s PTIN Renewal Time

Penalty relief sought for estates, trusts

  • The AICPA wants the IRS to waive 2026 estimated tax penalties for certain estates and trusts.
  • The group flags open questions on how a provision fits with the rules specific to estates and trusts.
  • Fiduciaries should watch for IRS guidance before making final 2026 payment decisions.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: AICPA Calls on IRS to Waive 2026 Estimated Tax Penalties for Certain Estates and Trusts

Voters reshape state taxes

  • Washington's income tax on high earners survives after backers of repeal measure Initiative 645 conceded.
  • Colorado supporters claim victory for Amendment 87, which would raise income taxes on high earners.
  • Florida voters approved cutting property taxes, leaving local governments weighing which services to trim.
