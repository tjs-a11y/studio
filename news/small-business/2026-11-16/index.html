Monday, November 16, 2026 · Small Business Money Brief

SBA Streamlines Surety Bond Guarantee Program; IRS Corrects Opportunity Zone Reporting Plan

A final SBA rule cuts surety paperwork, the IRS corrects proposed QOZ reporting rules, and a tax credit hearing moves to phone only.

The SBA finalized a rule cutting document submission and retention burdens in its Surety Bond Guarantee program, a lifeline for small contractors who need bonding.

SBA Surety Bond Rule Finalized

  • The SBA issued a final rule modernizing its Surety Bond Guarantee program regulations.
  • Participating surety companies face lighter document submission and retention requirements, which could ease bonding for small contractors.
  • The rule also fixes conflicting provisions in the program's regulations.
  • Outdated Preferred Surety admissions requirements and the Quarterly Contract Completion Report are being revised.

Source: Federal Register: SBA: Rule: Surety Bond Guarantee Program: Streamlining and Modernizing

IRS Corrects QOZ Reporting Proposal

  • The IRS published corrections to proposed regulations REG-116506-25, first released on September 11, 2026.
  • Qualified opportunity funds would file information returns and send statements to investors who dispose of their investments.
  • Qualified opportunity zone businesses would also furnish statements to the funds holding interests in them.
  • Bookkeepers serving QOZ businesses should note these new statement duties as the rules take shape.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Information Reporting Regarding Qualified Opportunity Zones and Updated Qualified Opportunity Fund Certification and Decertification Procedures; Correction

Scholarship Credit Hearing Goes Phone-Only

  • The December 15, 2026 hearing on the federal scholarship tax credit proposal is now telephonic only.
  • The proposal, REG-117199-25, covers a non-refundable credit for qualified contributions to scholarship granting organizations.
  • Clients planning such contributions should track the guidance as it develops.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Federal Scholarship Tax Credit; Hearing

IRS E-Filing Panel Adds Members

  • The IRS named 10 new members to the Electronic Tax Administration Advisory Committee.
  • The committee, created by statute in 1998, serves as a public forum on electronic tax administration issues.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: IRS Adds 10 New Members to ETAAC
