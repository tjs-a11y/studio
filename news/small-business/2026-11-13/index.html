IRS Corrects Opportunity Zone Reporting Proposal, Moves Scholarship Credit Hearing to Phone

Corrections land on new opportunity fund reporting rules, a December 15 hearing goes telephonic, and the IRS asks for burden comments.

The IRS issued corrections to its proposed opportunity zone information-reporting rules. Funds, zone businesses and their investors face new statement and filing duties.

Opportunity Zone Reporting Rules Corrected

  • The IRS published corrections to proposed regulations REG-116506-25, first released September 11, 2026.
  • Qualified opportunity funds would file information returns and send statements to investors who dispose of their stakes.
  • Zone businesses would also owe statements to funds holding interests in them, adding a new reporting layer for small operators.
  • The proposal also updates how funds get certified and decertified, which matters for anyone raising or holding fund capital.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Information Reporting Regarding Qualified Opportunity Zones and Updated Qualified Opportunity Fund Certification and Decertification Procedures; Correction

Scholarship Credit Hearing Goes Phone-Only

  • The December 15, 2026 hearing at 10 a.m. ET on REG-117199-25 is now telephonic only.
  • The rules cover a non-refundable federal credit for donations to scholarship granting organizations.
  • Business owners weighing year-end giving should track this guidance as it takes shape.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Federal Scholarship Tax Credit; Hearing

IRS Wants Input on Withholding Systems

  • The IRS is seeking public comment on paperwork burden for its QI, WP and WT application and account system.
  • The request falls under the Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 and affects intermediaries handling foreign withholding.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Notice: [Proposed Extension of Information Collection Request Submitted for Public Comment; Comment Request on Burden Related to the Qualified Intermediary (QI), Withholding Foreign Partnership (WP), and Withholding Foreign Trust (WT) Application and Account Management System

Texas Climbs Tax Competitiveness Ranking

  • Texas rose two spots in the Tax Foundation's latest Tax Competitiveness rankings.
  • Its lack of a state income tax earned high marks, a factor for owners comparing where to base a business.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Texas Moves Up Two Spots in Lowest-Taxes Ranking
