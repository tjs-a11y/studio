Owner-Operators: Eight Dates to Calendar

  • Eight tax dates fall before March 1, 2027, for owner-operators, per a new calendar roundup.
  • The dates draw on federal rules, guidance from four states and the International Fuel Tax Agreement.
  • IFTA deadlines sit alongside federal ones, so fuel-tax filers juggle multiple deadline sets at once.
  • Mapping these dates now helps avoid penalties during the busy year-end and early-filing stretch.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: The Owner-Operator Tax Calendar: 8 Dates Before March 1, 2027

Tax Breaks That Die With the Owner

  • Net operating losses and passive activity losses can disappear permanently when a taxpayer dies.
  • Capital loss carryforwards and unused charitable contribution carryforwards face the same risk.
  • The step-up in basis can still deliver significant tax benefits to heirs.
  • Owners carrying business losses should review which attributes survive as part of succession planning.

Source: The CPA Journal: What Dies with the Taxpayer?

Federal Register: IRS Notices

  • The IRS Advisory Council will hold a public meeting, per a notice published Oct. 28.
  • Open meetings let practitioners track issues the council raises with IRS leadership.
  • The IRS also issued its quarterly list of individuals who have chosen to expatriate, dated Oct. 30.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Notice: Internal Revenue Service Advisory Council; Public Meeting · Federal Register: IRS: Notice: Quarterly Publication of Individuals, Who Have Chosen To Expatriate
