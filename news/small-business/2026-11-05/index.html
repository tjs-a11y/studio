PTIN Renewal Window Is Open

  • Current preparer tax identification numbers expire Dec. 31, 2026, so renewal is now on the year-end checklist.
  • Anyone paid to prepare returns needs a valid PTIN, and it must be renewed every year.
  • A lapsed PTIN could sideline preparers just as filing season approaches.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Attention Tax Pros: It’s PTIN Renewal Time

IRS Posts Hearing Notice on Trump Accounts

  • The IRS issued a hearing notice tied to proposed rules on eligible investments for Trump accounts.
  • The proposed regulations first appeared in the Federal Register on August 21, 2026.
  • The rules govern the only assets these accounts may hold before the year the beneficiary turns 18.
  • Advisers fielding family-savings questions should monitor how the final rules take shape.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Guidance on Eligible Investments for Trump Accounts; Hearing

Big Cities Push Taxes on the Wealthy

  • Chicago voters backed a millionaire tax in a non-binding referendum, and the mayor called it a mandate for tax-the-rich policies.
  • A Brooklyn appeals court reversed a lower court ruling, allowing New York City's pied-à-terre tax to move forward.
  • The ruling means the city does not have to restart the process, a key win for Mayor Zohran Mamdani.
  • Owners and clients with high incomes or second homes in these cities should watch for follow-up action.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Millionaire Tax Wins Broad Support From Chicago Voters · CPA Practice Advisor: Appeals Court Hands Mamdani Key Win in Allowing NYC Pied-à-Terre Tax to Move Forward
