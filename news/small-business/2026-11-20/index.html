IRS Finalizes Auto-Enrollment Rules as Court Says Fraud Removes Audit Time Limit

Final Section 414A retirement plan rules, no audit deadline when fraud occurs, mandatory R&D credit reporting and a W-9 check that heads off 1099 trouble.

The IRS has issued final regulations on automatic enrollment for certain retirement plans, putting SECURE 2.0 requirements into effect for employers that maintain them.

Auto-enrollment rules are final

  • Final Section 414A regulations set automatic enrollment requirements for certain retirement plans; sponsoring employers should review their plan terms.
  • The rules carry out the SECURE 2.0 Act of 2022 mandate for eligible automatic contribution arrangements, so many salary-deferral plans must comply.
  • Employers maintaining these plans are directly affected, as are plan participants and beneficiaries.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Rule: Automatic Enrollment Requirements Under Section 414A

Fraud erases the IRS clock

  • The IRS usually gets three years to challenge a return, or six in certain instances, a window owners often rely on.
  • A new case says no time limit applies when fraud occurred, leaving those returns open to challenge indefinitely.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: No Tax Protection Allowed if Fraud Is Committed

R&D credit reporting gets stricter

  • Business-component reporting on Form 6765 is mandatory for 2026 tax-year returns, so credit claimants need more detailed records.
  • Year-end studies based on spreadsheets and staff interviews may not fit the new Section G demands, so firms should rework data collection.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Section G Retires the Year-End R&D Credit Study. Here’s What CPA Firms Should Change Now

Check W-9s before 1099 season

  • A vendor W-9 can look complete and properly stored yet still hold a mismatched name and TIN that goes unnoticed.
  • That mismatch can land on a Form 1099 and later prompt an IRS notice, so verify vendor names and TINs now.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: The W-9 Problem Your Busy Season Workflow Audit Can Miss
