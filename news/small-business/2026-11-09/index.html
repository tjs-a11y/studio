Trump Account Rules Head to Hearing

  • A Federal Register notice dated 2026-11-04 concerns a hearing on proposed Trump account investment rules.
  • The underlying proposal (CC- 00349938-26) first appeared in the Federal Register on August 21, 2026.
  • Only eligible investments may be held before the beginning of the calendar year in which the beneficiary turns 18.
  • This is still a proposal, so final investment limits could change before the rules take effect.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Guidance on Eligible Investments for Trump Accounts; Hearing

IRS Tax Forum Seminars Go Online

  • The IRS has launched its 2026 Nationwide Tax Forum Online for tax professionals.
  • More than a dozen seminars recorded at this year's in-person forum are included.
  • Tax pros can earn continuing education credits remotely, which matters for credential upkeep.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: IRS Offers Continuing Education Credits Via Nationwide Tax Forum Online Seminars
