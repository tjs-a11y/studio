Pittsburgh Proposal Would Tax Investment and Rental Income at 2%

A Pittsburgh tax plan targets dividends and rents, trucking clients face IFTA record rules, and big installment sales carry an interest charge.

A Pittsburgh council member has proposed a 2% tax on unearned income such as dividends, royalties, property sales and some rental profits.

Pittsburgh Floats New 2% Tax

  • Councilwoman Barb Warwick introduced a bill adding a 2% levy on dividends, royalties and property sale proceeds.
  • Some rental property income could also be taxed, a concern for local landlords and real estate investors.
  • The proposal comes as the city faces a dire financial outlook, so the debate may move quickly.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: With Pittsburgh Facing a Dire Financial Future, One Council Member Wants to Enact a New Tax

Trucking Clients: Keep IFTA Records

  • The IFTA Procedures Manual requires licensees to keep the distance and fuel records behind each return for four years.
  • Missing mileage or fuel logs can leave carriers exposed if a fuel tax audit arrives.
  • Bookkeepers serving trucking firms should confirm those records are complete and retrievable now.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: The Fuel Tax Audit Your Trucking Client is Not Ready For

Big Installment Sales Carry a Cost

  • Once a seller's installment obligations for the year exceed $5 million, Section 453A adds an annual interest charge on deferred tax.
  • Owners selling a business on installment terms should model that charge before closing the deal.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Structured Installment Sales Over $5 Million: What CPAs Should Model Before Closing

IRS Regulatory Watch

  • The IRS partially withdrew proposed Section 355 rules on the device test and active trade or business requirement.
  • Those rules govern tax-free spin-offs of controlled corporation stock, affecting corporations and their shareholders.
  • The IRS Advisory Council has announced a public meeting, per a Federal Register notice.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Guidance Under Section 355 Concerning Device and Active Trade or Business; Partial Withdrawal · Federal Register: IRS: Notice: Internal Revenue Service Advisory Council; Public Meeting
