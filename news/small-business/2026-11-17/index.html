Tuesday, November 17, 2026 · Small Business Money Brief

SBA Raises SBIC Leverage Limits and Streamlines Surety Bond Guarantee Rules

Two SBA rules touch small-business financing, the IRS corrects opportunity zone reporting plans, and Florida preparer fraud cases mount.

SBA issued an interim final rule letting SBICs issuing standard debentures access more leverage. That could widen the pool of investment capital for small firms.

SBIC leverage ceiling goes up

  • SBA published an interim final rule on SBIC program loan guarantee regulations.
  • It implements the Investing in All of America Act of 2025, which Congress passed to expand SBIC financing.
  • SBICs issuing standard debentures can get more leverage, in ratios up to two-to-one.
  • More fund leverage could mean more capital available to small businesses seeking investment.

Source: Federal Register: SBA: Rule: Small Business Investment Company (SBIC) Leverage Availability

Surety bond program gets a cleanup

  • SBA finalized changes to its Surety Bond Guarantee program regulations.
  • Participating surety companies face lighter document submission and retention requirements.
  • The rule fixes conflicting provisions and updates outdated Preferred Surety admission requirements.
  • The Quarterly Contract Completion Report is also revised, relevant to contractors relying on SBA-backed bonds.

Source: Federal Register: SBA: Rule: Surety Bond Guarantee Program: Streamlining and Modernizing

IRS reporting and hearing updates

  • The IRS corrected proposed regulations REG-116506-25 on qualified opportunity zone information reporting.
  • Those rules would require opportunity funds to file information returns and furnish statements to investors who dispose of interests.
  • Opportunity zone businesses would furnish statements to the funds holding interests in them.
  • The December 15, 2026 hearing on the scholarship tax credit proposal is now telephonic-only.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Information Reporting Regarding Qualified Opportunity Zones and Updated Qualified Opportunity Fund Certification and Decertification Procedures; Correction · Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Federal Scholarship Tax Credit; Hearing

Florida preparers face fraud actions

  • Federal authorities have brought a series of enforcement actions against Florida tax preparers.
  • Preparers are accused of inflating refunds with invented business losses, expenses and deductions.
  • Clients whose returns carry fabricated deductions can be left exposed to the IRS.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Made-Up Deductions and Clients Left Exposed to the IRS: Florida Tax Preparer Fraud Uncovered
