Sunday, November 15, 2026 · Small Business Money Brief

IRS Corrects Opportunity Zone Reporting Plan; SBA Streamlines Surety Bond Program

The IRS fixes proposed Opportunity Zone reporting rules, SBA finalizes surety bond changes, and a tax credit hearing moves to phone-only.

The IRS issued corrections to proposed rules requiring new information returns and investor statements for Opportunity Zone funds and businesses.

Opportunity Zone reporting rules corrected

  • The IRS corrected proposed regulations REG-116506-25, first published September 11, 2026.
  • Qualified opportunity funds would file information returns and send statements to investors who sell their stakes.
  • Qualified opportunity zone businesses would owe statements to funds holding interests in them, adding a new reporting duty.
  • Bookkeepers for zone businesses should watch for added year-end paperwork once the rules are finalized.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Information Reporting Regarding Qualified Opportunity Zones and Updated Qualified Opportunity Fund Certification and Decertification Procedures; Correction

SBA eases surety bond paperwork

  • SBA finalized changes to its Surety Bond Guarantee program regulations.
  • Participating surety companies face lighter document submission and retention requirements.
  • The rule also fixes conflicting provisions and revises outdated Preferred Surety admission rules and the Quarterly Contract Completion Report.
  • Small contractors that rely on SBA-backed bonds may see a smoother process with their sureties.

Source: Federal Register: SBA: Rule: Surety Bond Guarantee Program: Streamlining and Modernizing

Scholarship credit hearing goes phone-only

  • The December 15, 2026 hearing on REG-117199-25 will now be held by telephone only.
  • The proposal covers a non-refundable federal credit for contributions to scholarship granting organizations.
  • Owners weighing such donations can follow the guidance as it develops.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Federal Scholarship Tax Credit; Hearing
