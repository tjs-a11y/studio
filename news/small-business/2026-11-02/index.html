State Tax Votes on High Earners Advance as Supreme Court Takes Up Distilling Ban

California and Colorado tax measures move forward, the Supreme Court reviews a federal distilling ban, and a film credit push hits the lame duck.

Voters in California and Colorado are weighing higher taxes on top earners. Colorado's highest court just cleared the way for its measure to proceed.

High-Earner Tax Measures Move Forward

  • Colorado's Supreme Court threw out a suit to void the vote on Amendment 87, a tax hike on the wealthiest residents.
  • California's Proposition 3 would make higher rates permanent above $371,000 for singles and $742,000 for couples.
  • Prop 3 looks likely to pass, while California's separate billionaire wealth tax is struggling.
  • Owners of some top California sports teams would face the wealth tax if voters approve it.

Supreme Court Tests Federal Taxing Power

  • The Supreme Court will hear a challenge to a 158-year-old federal ban on home distilling.
  • The core question is whether the ban goes beyond Congress's power to tax.
  • The ruling could clarify how far federal tax authority reaches into regulating activity.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Centuries-Old Whiskey Tax Law Faces New Supreme Court Challenge

Film Tax Credit Pushed in Lame Duck

  • A bipartisan group is pressing to pass a proposed federal film tax credit before the 119th Congress ends.
  • Only weeks remain in the session, so the proposal faces a tight window.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Backers of Federal Film Tax Credit Seek Action in Lame-Duck Session

Year-End Payroll and Rental Sales

  • Many errors that complicate the final 2026 payroll close have already occurred, so reviewing records now matters.
