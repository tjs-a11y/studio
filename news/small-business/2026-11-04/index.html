State Tax Measures: Early Results

  • Washington: voters are keeping the new high-earners income tax and rejecting a repeal, which matters for profitable owners there.
  • California: a proposed wealth tax on billionaires was behind in early returns after heavy opposition spending.
  • Colorado: a plan to replace the flat income tax with tiered rates is too close to call.
  • Florida: Amendment 3 passed, sharply cutting property taxes on owner-occupied homes and bringing state budget cuts.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: California Billionaire Tax Trailing in Early Returns · CPA Practice Advisor: Washington Voters Upholding ‘Millionaires Tax’ in Tuesday Vote · CPA Practice Advisor: Colorado’s Tiered Income Tax Proposal Now Too Close to Call · CPA Practice Advisor: Florida’s Amendment 3 Passes, Bringing Property Tax Change and Budget Cuts

IRS Posts Record Collections

  • $5.6 trillion: total taxes the IRS collected in the fiscal year, per its Oct. 29 report.
  • That haul rose nearly 5% from 2025, a record for the agency.
  • Refunds also grew larger in 2026, a useful benchmark when setting client expectations.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: IRS Reports Record Tax Collections, Larger Refunds in 2026

Trump Account Investment Rules

  • The IRS has scheduled a hearing on proposed rules defining eligible investments for Trump accounts.
  • The proposal was first published in the Federal Register on August 21, 2026.
  • Until the year the beneficiary turns 18, the account may hold only these eligible investments.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Guidance on Eligible Investments for Trump Accounts; Hearing
