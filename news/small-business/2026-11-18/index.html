Wednesday, November 18, 2026 · Small Business Money Brief

IRS Finalizes 401(k) and 403(b) Auto-Enrollment Rules; SBA Revises SBIC and Surety Bond Programs

Employers get final SECURE 2.0 auto-enrollment guidance, while SBA expands SBIC leverage and simplifies its surety bond guarantee program.

Treasury and the IRS have issued final regulations on SECURE 2.0's automatic enrollment requirement for 401(k) and 403(b) plans. Employers sponsoring these plans should review how the rules apply to them.

Auto-enrollment rules are now final

  • Treasury and the IRS issued final regulations implementing SECURE 2.0's automatic enrollment requirement for 401(k) and 403(b) plans.
  • Certain cash or deferred arrangements must be eligible automatic contribution arrangements that meet additional requirements, so plan design may need updating.
  • The rules affect employers maintaining these plans as well as participants and beneficiaries, making them relevant for payroll and benefits setup.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Rule: Automatic Enrollment Requirements Under Section 414A · CPA Practice Advisor: IRS Issues Final Automatic Enrollment Regs for 401(k) and 403(b) Plans

SBA raises SBIC leverage limits

  • An SBA interim final rule implements the Investing in All of America Act of 2025 for Small Business Investment Companies.
  • SBICs issuing standard debentures can access more leverage, at ratios of up to two-to-one against private capital.
  • More leverage for these funds could expand the investment capital available to small businesses.

Source: Federal Register: SBA: Rule: Small Business Investment Company (SBIC) Leverage Availability

Surety bond program gets streamlined

  • An SBA final rule reduces document submission and retention burdens for surety companies in the Surety Bond Guarantee program.
  • Less paperwork for sureties may ease bonding for small contractors who rely on SBA-backed bonds.
  • The rule also fixes conflicting provisions and revises outdated Preferred Surety admissions and Quarterly Contract Completion Report requirements.

Source: Federal Register: SBA: Rule: Surety Bond Guarantee Program: Streamlining and Modernizing

Scholarship tax credit hearing moves to phone

  • The December 15, 2026 hearing on proposed rules for the federal scholarship tax credit will be held by telephone only.
  • The proposed rules cover the non-refundable credit for qualified contributions to scholarship granting organizations.
  • Regulators say they intend to allow scholarship funds to cover a broad range of K-12 educational needs.

Source: Federal Register: IRS: Proposed Rule: Federal Scholarship Tax Credit; Hearing · CPA Practice Advisor: IRS Will Go ‘As Broad As We Can’ With Federal K-12 Choice Rules
