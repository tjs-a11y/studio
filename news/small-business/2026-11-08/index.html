2027 tax numbers are out

  • Nearly 70 provisions got tax year 2027 inflation adjustments from the IRS, so planning can now use official figures.
  • Income tax brackets and the standard deduction are among the updated items, affecting owners' and freelancers' 2027 projections.
  • Bookkeepers should refresh 2027 estimate and payroll planning templates once they review the new IRS tables.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: IRS Adjusts Tax Brackets, Standard Deduction for Tax Year 2027

New opportunity zone map

  • More than 6,500 communities now hold federal opportunity zone status, according to Treasury and the IRS.
  • The designations span all 50 states, the District of Columbia and five U.S. territories.
  • Owners weighing where to invest or expand should check whether their area made the new list.

Source: CPA Practice Advisor: Treasury, IRS Designate Over 6,500 Communities as Opportunity Zones

Ballot results reshape state taxes

  • Florida voters approved Amendment 3, cutting property taxes; local governments now face decisions on which services to trim.
  • Colorado backers of Amendment 87 claim victory for a rewrite that would lift income taxes on high earners.
  • Washington's high-earner income tax stands after Initiative 645, the repeal effort, failed.
  • Business owners in these states should watch for rollout details that could change their tax bills.

Penalty relief sought for some trusts

  • The AICPA wants the IRS to waive 2026 estimated tax penalties for certain estates and trusts.
  • Uncertainty lingers over how a provision fits with the rules that specifically govern estates and trusts.
  • Practitioners handling fiduciary returns should watch for IRS guidance before estimated payments come due.
