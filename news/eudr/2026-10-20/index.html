JRC updates 2020 forest map

  • Version 4 of the JRC Global Forest Cover map for 2020 is now out, so screening teams should note which version they use.
  • The European Commission's environment newsroom announced the release on 2026-10-19, flagging it as an official EU update.
  • Exporters and cooperatives should confirm which map version their geolocation checks rely on, since results may differ between versions.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: The JRC released new Version 4 of the Global Forest Cover map for the year 2020

Cocoa: worst may be past

  • Barry Callebaut says the cocoa crisis has likely peaked, offering cautious relief to buyers and suppliers.
  • APAC cocoa players still face major market shifts and challenges, so supply-chain risk has not gone away.

Source: FoodNavigator: APAC cocoa trends: ‘Worst is past’, but risks remain
