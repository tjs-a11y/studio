Who is actually ready to comply?

  • Mongabay launched a special issue tracking how industries are adapting to EUDR traceability demands and where they fall short.
  • Coverage spans soy traders in Brazil, cocoa firms in Ghana and palm oil smallholders in Southeast Asia.
  • The series lands as the regulation edges toward enforcement following multiple delays, making peer benchmarks timely for exporters.

Source: Mongabay: Who’s implementing EUDR?

Chocolate makers hedge on cocoa supply

  • Manufacturers are turning to cocoa alternatives, AI and new ingredients to manage volatility and supply strain.
  • For cocoa exporters and cooperatives, shifting buyer strategies could reshape demand alongside EUDR traceability pressure.
