November training for EUDR filings

  • The Commission released November training dates for the EUDR Information System on 2026-10-22.
  • The Information System is where due-diligence statements are filed, so compliance teams should plan attendance early.
  • The notice did not list specific session details, so check the official schedule directly.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: EU Deforestation Regulation Update - Information System Training dates for November

Commissioner joins EUDR event

  • An EU Commissioner attended an EUDR event, the Commission's environment newsroom reported on 2026-10-27.
  • No policy changes or new guidance were announced in the item, so current obligations stand unchanged.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: EUDR & Commissioner

EU forest and habitat snapshot

  • EU forest cover has stabilised at approximately 39% of total land, according to Commission figures.
  • 26.4% of EU land is protected, yet 81% of protected habitats show poor or bad conservation status.
  • These figures frame the EU's environmental case behind deforestation-linked trade rules.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: Did you know?
