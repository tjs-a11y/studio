Ghana's Timber License After 16 Years

  • Ghana completed a 16-year VPA process with the EU and now holds a license to sell sustainable timber.
  • Europe is a key market for Ghanaian timber, so the license matters directly to exporters shipping wood there.
  • The VPA offered Ghana both EU market access and support for stronger national forest governance.
  • Mongabay's analysis says the process improved institutions and cooperation, useful context for buyers assessing legality risk.

Source: Mongabay: How Ghana’s new license to sell sustainable timber improves institutions & cooperation (analysis)

Kraft Heinz Sells North American Coffee

  • Kraft Heinz will sell its North American coffee business to One Rock for $650 million.
  • Kraft Heinz will keep a 30% stake, so it remains tied to the business.
  • Cash from the sale will go toward cutting debt.
  • A new majority owner may mean new contacts and supplier expectations for coffee exporters selling into this business.

Source: FoodNavigator: Kraft Heinz to sell North American coffee business for $650M
