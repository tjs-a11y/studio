JRC forest map reaches Version 4

  • Version 4 of the Global Forest Cover map for 2020 has been released by the JRC.
  • The update was announced in the Commission's environment newsroom on 2026-10-19.
  • If your geolocation checks reference this map, record the version used in your due-diligence files.
  • The announcement does not detail what changed from earlier versions, so compare outputs before relying on past results.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: The JRC released new Version 4 of the Global Forest Cover map for the year 2020

November training for the Information System

  • The Commission has published November training dates for the EUDR Information System.
  • The notice was posted on 2026-10-22 as an EUDR update.
  • Staff who submit due-diligence statements should check the Commission's EUDR pages for the session schedule.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: EU Deforestation Regulation Update - Information System Training dates for November
