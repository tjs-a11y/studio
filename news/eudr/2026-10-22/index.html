JRC updates 2020 forest map

  • The JRC released Version 4 of its Global Forest Cover map for 2020, announced on 2026-10-19.
  • The map shows forest cover for 2020, so teams using it in plot-level geolocation checks should note the update.
  • Compliance staff should confirm which map version their screening tools and supplier assessments currently reference.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: The JRC released new Version 4 of the Global Forest Cover map for the year 2020

Heat adds pressure on cocoa

  • Rising temperatures are affecting both cocoa supplies and consumer demand for chocolate, FoodNavigator reports.
  • More frequent heatwaves are pushing manufacturers to adjust product formats, strategies and retail approaches.
  • Cocoa exporters and cooperatives face climate-driven supply risk alongside price pressure.

Source: FoodNavigator: Chocolate has a new problem. And it’s not cocoa prices
