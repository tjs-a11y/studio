PepsiCo reports 94% deforestation-free sourcing for key commodities in 2025

PepsiCo raises its share of forest-clearance-free ingredients like palm oil, while Starbucks reports record inventory availability.

PepsiCo says 94% of key ingredients, including palm oil, were sourced without forest clearance in 2025. That is up from 90% a year earlier.

PepsiCo raises deforestation-free share

  • 94% of key PepsiCo ingredients, such as palm oil and sugar cane, came from sources without forest clearance in 2025.
  • The prior year's figure was 90%, showing major buyers track deforestation-free sourcing year over year.
  • Palm oil is an EUDR commodity, so suppliers to big food brands face overlapping corporate and EU data demands.

Source: Supply Chain Dive: PepsiCo improves deforestation-free sourcing for key commodities

Starbucks touts record inventory availability

  • Starbucks' CEO says store inventory availability is at an all-time high, supported by daily deliveries.
  • The chain is testing a 24-hour operating model aimed at a faster, more dependable supply chain.
  • The report covers logistics only and gives no detail on coffee sourcing or deforestation checks.

Source: Supply Chain Dive: Starbucks inventory availability at ‘all-time high’ with daily deliveries, CEO says
