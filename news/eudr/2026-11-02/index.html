EU Forest Cover Holds at 39%

  • EU forest cover has stabilised at approximately 39% of total land, per Commission figures.
  • 26.4% of total EU land is designated as protected.
  • 81% of protected EU habitats have a poor or bad conservation status.
  • These figures concern forests inside the EU, not the producer-country supply chains that EUDR due diligence covers.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: Did you know?
