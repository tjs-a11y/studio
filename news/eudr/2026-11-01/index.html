Quiet EUDR week: Commission data on forests, cocoa replacers gain ground

No new EUDR rules this week, but EU forest data and cocoa cost pressures offer context for commodity supply chains.

No new EUDR deadlines or guidance surfaced this week. The Commission's own figures put EU forest cover at approximately 39% of land.

Commission keeps EUDR in view

  • A Commissioner attended an EUDR event, per a 2026-10-27 Commission environment notice; no new rules or guidance were reported.
  • EU forest cover has stabilised at approximately 39% of total land, according to the Commission.
  • 26.4% of EU land is designated as protected, yet 81% of protected habitats are in poor or bad condition.
  • These figures frame the EU's push to curb deforestation, including through import rules on commodities.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: EUDR & Commissioner · European Commission: Environment newsroom: Did you know?

Cocoa substitutes target cost pressure

  • A new high-fiber cocoa powder replacer can substitute up to 100% of cocoa in select uses.
  • Its maker cites rising cocoa costs and supply risks as drivers for the launch.
  • Cocoa exporters and cooperatives should watch whether such substitutes erode demand as compliance and price pressures grow.

Source: FoodNavigator: New cocoa powder alternative targets GLP-1 trends and supply risks

Alternative proteins chase scale

  • Aleph Farms and Mottainai Food Tech each secured S$1m to scale cultivated meat and fermented protein.
  • Both target lower costs and supply resilience, relevant to cattle and soy markets under EUDR scrutiny.
  • Funding remains small, so near-term impact on traditional cattle and soy trade is limited.

Source: FoodNavigator: From cultivated beef to fermented soy: Bezos-backed protein innovators target cost and scale
