Ghana gets EU timber licence

  • Ghana now has a licence to sell sustainable timber, a key credential for wood exporters targeting EU buyers.
  • The licence follows a 16-year Voluntary Partnership Agreement (VPA) process with the European Union.
  • Europe is a major market for Ghanaian timber, so legality credentials directly affect market access.
  • The VPA also aimed to strengthen national forest governance, which shapes how exporters document legal sourcing.

Source: Mongabay: How Ghana’s new license to sell sustainable timber improves institutions & cooperation (analysis)

Kraft Heinz exits North American coffee

  • Kraft Heinz will sell its North American coffee business to One Rock for $650 million.
  • Kraft Heinz keeps a 30% stake, so coffee suppliers face a partial change in buyer ownership.
  • Kraft Heinz plans to use the cash proceeds to cut debt.

Source: FoodNavigator: Kraft Heinz to sell North American coffee business for $650M
