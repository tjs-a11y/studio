EU forests steady, habitats struggling

  • EU forest cover has stabilised at approximately 39% of total land, per the Commission's environment newsroom.
  • 26.4% of total EU land is designated as protected, framing the EU's domestic conservation baseline.
  • 81% of protected EU habitats have a poor or bad conservation status, a gap critics of EUDR enforcement may highlight.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: Did you know?

Commissioner appears at EUDR event

  • The Commission's environment newsroom reports a Commissioner attended an EUDR event.
  • No details on announcements, guidance or timelines were published, so compliance plans need no change yet.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: EUDR & Commissioner

Cocoa replacer targets supply risk

  • Planet A Foods launched Cocoa Booster, a high-fiber cocoa powder replacer.
  • It can replace up to 100% of cocoa in select applications, a signal for cocoa exporters watching demand.
  • The launch cites rising cocoa costs and GLP-1-driven shifts in consumer demand.

Source: FoodNavigator: New cocoa powder alternative targets GLP-1 trends and supply risks
