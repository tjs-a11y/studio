Ghana's EU timber deal under review

  • Ghana spent 16 years negotiating a Voluntary Partnership Agreement with the EU before reaching its licensing milestone last year.
  • Europe is a key buyer of Ghanaian timber, so legality credentials directly shape access for wood exporters.
  • Beyond market access, the VPA brought support for stronger national forest governance, which the analysis says improved cooperation.
  • Note that this is an analysis piece, so treat its conclusions as interpretation rather than confirmed outcomes.

Source: Mongabay: How Ghana’s new license to sell sustainable timber improves institutions & cooperation (analysis)

Kraft Heinz sells coffee unit for $650M

  • Kraft Heinz is selling its North American coffee business to One Rock for $650 million.
  • Kraft Heinz will keep a 30% stake and use the cash to cut debt.
  • Coffee suppliers selling to this business should watch for a change in buyer ownership and possible shifts in sourcing contacts.

Source: FoodNavigator: Kraft Heinz to sell North American coffee business for $650M

Cocoa alternatives gain ground

  • Fermentation, cocoa alternatives and cultured cacao are giving bakery and snack makers new options for flavour, formulation and supply.
  • Cocoa exporters should track these substitutes because they could change long-term demand from snack manufacturers.

Source: FoodNavigator: Can science keep up with chocolate’s ambitions?
