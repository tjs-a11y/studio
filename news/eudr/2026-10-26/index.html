Commission Posts November EUDR Information System Training Schedule

Brussels posts November training dates for the EUDR Information System, and chocolate makers look beyond cocoa amid supply strain.

The European Commission has published November training dates for the EUDR Information System, the platform operators use to file due-diligence statements.

November EUDR system training announced

  • The Commission's environment newsroom posted an EUDR update on 22 October listing November training dates for the Information System.
  • Exporters, importers and compliance teams filing due-diligence statements should check the schedule and book seats early.
  • The update gives no further detail on content, so watch for follow-up guidance from the Commission.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: EU Deforestation Regulation Update - Information System Training dates for November

Chocolate makers hedge against cocoa strain

  • Manufacturers are testing cocoa alternatives, AI and new ingredients to cope with volatility, affordability and supply problems.
  • For cocoa cooperatives and traders, this signals buyers seeking ways to reduce reliance on beans.
  • Supply pressure makes reliable, traceable sourcing a stronger selling point for compliant cocoa origins.

Source: FoodNavigator: Beyond cocoa: The race to futureproof chocolate
