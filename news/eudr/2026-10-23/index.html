JRC Releases Version 4 of 2020 Global Forest Cover Map; EUDR System Training Set for November

The Commission publishes a new 2020 forest cover map version and announces November training dates for the EUDR Information System.

The EU's Joint Research Centre has released Version 4 of its Global Forest Cover map for 2020, a reference layer many EUDR teams use when checking plot locations.

New 2020 Forest Map Version

  • The JRC released Version 4 of its Global Forest Cover map for 2020, published by the Commission on 2026-10-19.
  • Teams that screen plot geolocation against this map should confirm which version their tools and suppliers currently use.
  • Results from earlier versions may differ, so past risk checks could need a fresh look.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: The JRC released new Version 4 of the Global Forest Cover map for the year 2020

November Information System Training

  • The Commission has announced November training dates for the EUDR Information System.
  • Staff who file due-diligence statements can use the sessions to learn the system before submissions pile up.
  • Check the Commission's environment newsroom for the session schedule and details.

Source: European Commission: Environment newsroom: EU Deforestation Regulation Update - Information System Training dates for November
