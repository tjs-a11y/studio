Saturday, October 17, 2026 · Scam Watch Brief

FBI Flags Midterm Election Scams; FTC Halts Alleged Sextortion 'Relief' Scheme

Election-season fraud warnings, a court halt on a sextortion help firm, $9.8 million in wire-fraud refunds, and a ticket broker lawsuit.

The FBI says scammers are exploiting the 2026 midterm election to run multiple fraud schemes. Expect political-season pitches to keep coming as November nears.

Midterm Election Scams on the Rise

  • The FBI warned on Oct. 15 that scammers are using the 2026 midterm election to run multiple fraud schemes.
  • A separate FBI alert cautions voters about election crimes ahead of the November vote.
  • Election season brings a flood of calls, texts and emails, which gives fraudsters cover to blend in.
  • Verify any election-related request before sharing money or personal details.

Source: FBI IC3 alerts: Scammers Exploit 2026 U.S. Midterm Election to Perpetrate Multiple Fraud Schemes · FBI IC3 alerts: FBI Warns Voters on Election Crimes Ahead of the November 2026 Midterm Election

Sextortion 'Help' Firm Halted

  • A federal court temporarily halted Digital Forensics Corporation LLC and its CEO at the FTC's request.
  • The FTC alleges the firm took millions of dollars with false promises to identify sextortion criminals and stop attacks.
  • The FTC calls sextortion a rapidly growing threat, often run by foreign scammers.
  • Victims in a panic can be targeted a second time by paid 'rescue' services that overpromise.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues Operator of Sextortion Relief Scheme for Misleading Consumers into Believing it Would Identify Criminals and End Sextortion Attacks

$9.8 Million Back for Wire-Fraud Victims

  • The FTC is sending more than $9.8 million to consumers harmed through Walmart's wire transfer services.
  • Scammers used those services to take hundreds of millions of dollars from U.S. consumers, according to the FTC.
  • Walmart settled FTC charges in June 2025 over allegedly weak anti-fraud policies and staff training.
  • Wire transfers are a favorite scam payment method because money is hard to recover once sent.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sends More than $9.8 Million to Consumers Harmed by Walmart’s Failure to Stop Scammers that Used its Wire Transfer Services to Take Millions from Customers

Ticket Broker Sued Over Bulk Buying

  • The FTC sued REPS & Company Inc., alleging it dodged limits on how many tickets one buyer can purchase.
  • The broker allegedly bought millions of tickets to high-demand events and resold them at a significant profit.
  • Such tactics can leave fans paying inflated resale prices for popular shows.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues Ticket Broker Over Unlawful Tactics Used to Buy Millions of Tickets to Popular Live Events
