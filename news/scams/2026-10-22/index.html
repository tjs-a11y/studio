Thursday, October 22, 2026 · Scam Watch Brief

FTC Targets Passport-Service Impersonators and Shuts Down Debt Relief Scheme

Regulators act against operations posing as the government to sell passport help and debt relief to seniors and veterans.

The FTC is suing companies it says posed as the federal government to sell passport services through paid search ads. If you need a passport, be wary of ads that look official.

Fake 'government' passport help

  • The FTC sued GovAssist LLC, Entry LLC and Scott Zuckerman over an alleged government impersonation scheme.
  • The operation allegedly targeted people seeking passport services by claiming to be, or be tied to, the federal government.
  • Paid search ads were allegedly used to mislead, so the top result is not always the official agency.
  • The FTC also cites misleading promises and fake reviews, so glowing ratings alone don't prove a service is legitimate.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues Perpetrators of Government Impersonation Scheme Over Deceptive Ads, Misleading Promises and Fake Reviews

Debt relief scheme permanently banned

  • A federal court permanently banned operators of an alleged debt relief scheme, at the FTC's request.
  • The scheme allegedly targeted seniors and veterans by posing as banks, card companies and government agencies.
  • On October 14, 2026, the court entered a $106.8 million default judgment against the corporate defendants.
  • Unsolicited debt-help offers that claim to come from your bank or an agency deserve extra scrutiny.

Source: FTC press releases: Court Permanently Shuts Down Illegal Debt Relief Operation Following FTC Allegation That Scheme’s Operators Impersonated Government and Business Entities

FTC reports on fraud against older adults

  • The FTC sent Congress its report, Protecting Older Consumers 2025-2026.
  • It draws on fraud reports filed by older adults to highlight key scam trends.
  • It also outlines enforcement actions aimed at deceptive practices hurting older Americans, useful context for families watching out for parents.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Issues Annual Report to Congress on Agency’s Actions to Protect Older Adults
