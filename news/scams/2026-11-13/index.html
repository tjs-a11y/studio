Friday, November 13, 2026 · Scam Watch Brief

Court Freezes Phantom Debt Operation; Family Dollar to Pay $63 Million

A federal court halts a debt collector accused of faking legal ties, while the FTC acts on health-data sharing and misleading prices.

At the FTC's request, a federal court froze a debt collection operation accused of pressuring people to pay debts they often did not owe.

Phantom Debt Collectors Halted

  • A federal court temporarily shut down the operation and froze its assets after an FTC request.
  • The collectors allegedly posed as a law firm or claimed ties to attorneys to scare people into paying.
  • They allegedly threatened to contact targets' employers and family members, a classic pressure tactic.
  • Many of the debts they chased were frequently not owed at all, according to the FTC.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Action Results in Court Issuing Temporary Restraining Order Halting Phantom Debt Collection Operation

Health Data Shared With Advertisers

  • Five states have joined the FTC's lawsuit against telehealth provider Hims and Hers.
  • The FTC alleges it passed sensitive health information to third-party advertising platforms while promising privacy.
  • The complaint also claims users were misled about billing and cancellation practices.

Source: FTC press releases: Five States Join FTC Lawsuit Alleging Hims and Hers Engaged in Deceptive and Unlawful Privacy Practices

Family Dollar Pays $63 Million

  • Family Dollar will pay $63 million to settle claims from the FTC and 17 states.
  • Shoppers were allegedly charged more at the register than shelf tags and ads showed.
  • The proposed order bars the chain from misrepresenting its prices, so checking receipts still pays off.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC and 17 States Secure $63 Million Settlement with Family Dollar Stores in Price Transparency Win for Consumers

Car Dealer Settles Over Bait Pricing

  • Stream Auto Outlet, a Long Island dealership group, settled FTC claims over misleading advertised prices.
  • The FTC said buyers ended up paying far more than the prices shown in ads.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Secures Settlement with Stream Auto Outlet, Advancing Price Transparency for Auto-Buying Consumers
