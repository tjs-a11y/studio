Saturday, October 24, 2026 · Scam Watch Brief

FTC targets passport-service impersonators as court shuts down debt relief scheme

Two FTC actions take aim at operations that posed as government agencies or banks, plus a new report on fraud against older adults.

Scammers posing as government agencies are in regulators' sights this week. The FTC sued an alleged passport-services impersonator and won a permanent ban against a debt relief scheme that targeted seniors.

Passport help that isn't the government

  • The FTC sued GovAssist LLC, Entry LLC and Scott Zuckerman over an alleged government impersonation scheme.
  • People seeking passport services were allegedly misled into thinking the operation was, or was tied to, the federal government.
  • The FTC says paid search ads were used to pose as the government, so top search results aren't proof a site is official.
  • The complaint also cites misleading promises and fake reviews, meaning glowing ratings shouldn't settle whether a service is legitimate.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues Perpetrators of Government Impersonation Scheme Over Deceptive Ads, Misleading Promises and Fake Reviews

Debt relief scheme permanently banned

  • A federal court permanently banned operators of an alleged debt relief scheme, acting at the FTC's request.
  • The scheme allegedly targeted seniors and veterans by posing as banks, credit card companies and government agencies.
  • On October 14, 2026, the court entered a $106.8 million default judgment against corporate defendants.
  • Unsolicited debt relief pitches claiming to come from a bank or agency deserve extra scrutiny, especially for older relatives.

Source: FTC press releases: Court Permanently Shuts Down Illegal Debt Relief Operation Following FTC Allegation That Scheme’s Operators Impersonated Government and Business Entities

New FTC report on older adults

  • The FTC sent Congress its Protecting Older Consumers 2025-2026 report on fraud affecting older Americans.
  • It draws on fraud reports filed by older adults to flag key trends in how they are targeted.
  • The report also details FTC enforcement against deceptive practices aimed at older consumers, useful context for families helping parents.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Issues Annual Report to Congress on Agency’s Actions to Protect Older Adults
