Sunday, October 25, 2026 · Scam Watch Brief

FTC Targets Government Impersonators in Passport and Debt Relief Schemes

A court shut down a debt relief scheme that preyed on seniors and veterans, and the FTC sued an alleged fake-government passport service.

Scammers posing as banks and government agencies are a top FTC target this week. A court entered a $106.8 million judgment against a debt relief operation aimed at seniors and veterans.

Debt Relief Scheme Permanently Shut Down

  • A federal court permanently banned operators of an alleged debt relief scheme, acting at the FTC's request.
  • The scheme targeted seniors and veterans, so older relatives and former service members are prime marks for this pitch.
  • Callers allegedly posed as banks, credit card companies and government agencies to seem trustworthy.
  • On October 14, 2026, the court entered a $106.8 million default judgment against the corporate defendants.

Source: FTC press releases: Court Permanently Shuts Down Illegal Debt Relief Operation Following FTC Allegation That Scheme’s Operators Impersonated Government and Business Entities

Passport Service Posed as the Government

  • The FTC sued GovAssist LLC, Entry LLC and Scott Zuckerman over an alleged government impersonation scheme.
  • The operation allegedly targeted people seeking passport services by claiming to be, or be tied to, the federal government.
  • Paid search ads were allegedly used to mimic the government, so a top search result is not proof of an official site.
  • The FTC also cites misleading promises and fake reviews, meaning glowing ratings can be manufactured.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues Perpetrators of Government Impersonation Scheme Over Deceptive Ads, Misleading Promises and Fake Reviews

FTC Reports to Congress on Older Adults

  • The FTC released Protecting Older Consumers 2025-2026, its latest report to Congress on fraud against older adults.
  • It highlights trends drawn from fraud reports filed by older adults themselves.
  • The report also details enforcement actions against unfair or deceptive practices hitting older Americans.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Issues Annual Report to Congress on Agency’s Actions to Protect Older Adults
