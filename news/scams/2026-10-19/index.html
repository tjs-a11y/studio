Monday, October 19, 2026 · Scam Watch Brief

FBI Flags Midterm Election Scams; FTC Halts Sextortion 'Relief' Scheme

The FBI warns of fraud tied to the 2026 midterms, a court halts a sextortion help service, and the FTC sends over $9.8 million to wire fraud victims.

The FBI says scammers are using the November 2026 midterm election to run multiple fraud schemes. Expect election-themed pitches to rise in the weeks ahead.

Midterms Bring a Wave of Scams

  • The FBI warned on October 15 that scammers are exploiting the 2026 midterm election with multiple fraud schemes.
  • A separate FBI alert warns voters about election crimes ahead of the November vote, so stay skeptical of unexpected election messages.
  • Election and campaign officials are also being targeted by cyber threat actors using a variety of techniques, the FBI said October 16.
  • Before acting on any election-related call, text or request, check it against official election sources first.

Sextortion 'Help' Service Halted

  • A federal court temporarily halted Digital Forensics Corporation LLC and its CEO at the FTC's request.
  • The FTC alleges the firm took millions of dollars from victims with false promises of sextortion assistance.
  • It allegedly claimed it could identify the criminals and stop the attacks, preying on people already being threatened.
  • Sextortion is a rapidly growing threat, often run by foreign scammers, so be wary of paid services promising to make it disappear.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues Operator of Sextortion Relief Scheme for Misleading Consumers into Believing it Would Identify Criminals and End Sextortion Attacks

Wire Fraud Victims Get Refunds

  • The FTC is sending more than $9.8 million to people scammed through Walmart's wire transfer services.
  • Scammers used those services to take hundreds of millions of dollars from U.S. consumers, the FTC said.
  • Walmart settled FTC charges in June 2025 over allegedly ineffective anti-fraud policies and procedures.
  • Anyone pressured to pay a stranger by wire transfer should treat it as a red flag.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sends More than $9.8 Million to Consumers Harmed by Walmart’s Failure to Stop Scammers that Used its Wire Transfer Services to Take Millions from Customers
