Dollar General accused of checkout overcharges

  • The FTC and 20 states sued Dollar General on October 26 over deceptive pricing.
  • Regulators allege register prices often topped shelf tags, price labels and ads for years.
  • The alleged practice cost shoppers millions of dollars, according to the complaint.
  • Compare your receipt against shelf prices, especially on frequent small purchases where gaps are easy to miss.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC and State Partners Sue Dollar General for Charging Consumers More than Advertised for Many Products

Toast sued over hidden fees

  • The FTC, California officials and seven state attorneys general sued Toast Inc. today.
  • The suit alleges hidden and unauthorized fees plus deceptive subscription and cancellation practices.
  • Colorado, Florida, Illinois, Iowa, Nebraska, Oklahoma and Utah joined the case.
  • Restaurant owners using the platform should review statements for charges they never agreed to.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC and States Take Action Against Toast Inc. over Hidden and Unauthorized Fees, Deceptive Subscription and Cancellation Practices and Other Unlawful Conduct

FTC probes data-driven pricing

  • The FTC ordered nine major airlines, ride-sharing and food-delivery firms to hand over detailed information.
  • The agency wants to know how personal data may shape what consumers pay and what gig workers earn.
  • If you see different prices than others for the same ride or flight, this inquiry targets that practice.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Launches Personalized Pricing and Compensation Inquiry
