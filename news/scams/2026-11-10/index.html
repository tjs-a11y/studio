Court Freezes Phantom Debt Collectors Posing as Lawyers, FTC Says

A court froze an alleged phantom debt operation, a landlord pays $49.75 million, and a car dealer settles over misleading advertised prices.

At the FTC's request, a federal court froze the assets of a debt collection operation accused of pressuring people to pay debts they often didn't owe.

Phantom Debt Collectors Shut Down

  • A federal court temporarily halted the operation and froze its assets after an FTC request.
  • Collectors allegedly posed as a law firm or claimed ties to attorneys to scare people into paying.
  • Threats to contact your employer or family were part of the alleged pressure campaign.
  • Many targeted consumers frequently did not owe the debts at all, so legal-sounding threats alone prove nothing.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Action Results in Court Issuing Temporary Restraining Order Halting Phantom Debt Collection Operation

Rental Giant Pays $49.75 Million

  • Progress Residential and owner Pretium Partners will pay $49.75 million to settle FTC and state charges.
  • The FTC and four states say renters were misled by deceptive rental pricing practices.
  • It is the largest enforcement matter the FTC has brought against a landlord, a signal for renters checking advertised rates.
  • Progress Residential is described as the nation's largest property manager of single-family rental homes.

Source: FTC press releases: Progress Residential and Owner Pretium Partners to Pay $49.75 Million to Settle FTC and States’ Charges Over Deceptive Rental Pricing Practices and Other Unlawful Conduct

Car Dealer Settles Over Price Ads

  • Stream Auto Outlet, a Long Island, New York dealership group, reached a settlement with the FTC.
  • The FTC said advertised prices were far lower than what buyers ultimately had to pay.
  • For car shoppers, the gap between the ad price and the final bill is worth checking before signing.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Secures Settlement with Stream Auto Outlet, Advancing Price Transparency for Auto-Buying Consumers
