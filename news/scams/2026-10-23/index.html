Friday, October 23, 2026 · Scam Watch Brief

FTC Targets Fake Passport Help Sites; Court Shuts Debt Relief Scheme

Regulators act against government impersonators selling passport services and a debt relief operation that targeted seniors and veterans.

The FTC has sued operators accused of posing as the government to sell passport services through paid search ads. Before paying for any passport help, confirm you are on an official government site.

Fake Government Passport Help

  • The FTC sued GovAssist LLC, Entry LLC and Scott Zuckerman over an alleged government impersonation scheme.
  • Consumers seeking passport services were allegedly misled into thinking the operation was, or was tied to, the federal government.
  • Paid search ads were allegedly used to impersonate the government, so top search results are not proof a site is official.
  • The FTC also cites misleading promises and fake reviews, so glowing ratings alone should not earn your trust.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues Perpetrators of Government Impersonation Scheme Over Deceptive Ads, Misleading Promises and Fake Reviews

Debt Relief Scheme Banned

  • A federal court permanently banned operators of an alleged debt relief scheme, following an FTC request.
  • The scheme allegedly targeted seniors and veterans, groups worth warning in your family.
  • Operators allegedly impersonated banks, credit card companies and government agencies to gain trust.
  • On October 14, 2026, the court entered a $106.8 million default judgment against corporate defendants.

Source: FTC press releases: Court Permanently Shuts Down Illegal Debt Relief Operation Following FTC Allegation That Scheme’s Operators Impersonated Government and Business Entities

FTC Report on Older Adults

  • The FTC sent Congress its report Protecting Older Consumers 2025-2026, built on fraud reports from older adults.
  • It highlights key fraud trends affecting older Americans, useful context for anyone helping aging parents.
  • The report details law enforcement actions against unfair or deceptive practices aimed at older consumers.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Issues Annual Report to Congress on Agency’s Actions to Protect Older Adults
