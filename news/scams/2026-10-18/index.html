Sunday, October 18, 2026 · Scam Watch Brief

FBI Warns of Midterm Election Scams as FTC Halts a Sextortion Relief Scheme

Fraudsters target the 2026 midterms, a court freezes a sextortion fixer, and Walmart wire-fraud victims get more than $9.8 million back.

The FBI says scammers are using the 2026 U.S. midterm election to run multiple fraud schemes. Expect more election-themed pitches as November nears.

Midterm election scams are rising

  • On Oct. 15, the FBI warned that scammers are exploiting the 2026 midterm election for multiple fraud schemes.
  • A separate FBI alert cautions voters about election crimes ahead of the November 2026 vote.
  • Treat unexpected election-themed calls, texts or donation requests with suspicion until you verify them on your own.

Source: FBI IC3 alerts: Scammers Exploit 2026 U.S. Midterm Election to Perpetrate Multiple Fraud Schemes · FBI IC3 alerts: FBI Warns Voters on Election Crimes Ahead of the November 2026 Midterm Election

Sextortion rescue service frozen by court

  • A federal court temporarily halted Digital Forensics Corporation LLC and its CEO at the FTC's request.
  • The FTC alleges the firm took millions of dollars by falsely promising to identify sextortion criminals and stop attacks.
  • Sextortion victims can be hit twice, so be wary of paid services claiming they can make the threats end.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sues Operator of Sextortion Relief Scheme for Misleading Consumers into Believing it Would Identify Criminals and End Sextortion Attacks

Walmart wire-fraud victims get refunds

  • The FTC is sending payments totaling more than $9.8 million to people scammed through Walmart's wire transfer services.
  • The agency alleged Walmart failed to stop scammers who took hundreds of millions of dollars from U.S. consumers.
  • Walmart settled the charges in June 2025, including claims it lacked effective anti-fraud policies.

Source: FTC press releases: FTC Sends More than $9.8 Million to Consumers Harmed by Walmart’s Failure to Stop Scammers that Used its Wire Transfer Services to Take Millions from Customers
