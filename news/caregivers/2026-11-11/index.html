Experts Target Gun Safety as Dementia Emerges; Midterms Reopen Medicare Debate

Public health experts focus on firearm safety for older gun owners with cognitive decline, and a new Congress revives the Medicare for All question.

Dementia and Guns at Home

  • Public health experts are developing approaches that encourage firearm safety among older adults who own guns.
  • The effort centers on households where a gun owner shows signs of cognitive decline, a situation many family caregivers face.
  • Families with concerns about a loved one's memory and home safety can raise the topic with that person's doctor.

Source: KFF Health News: When a Gun Owner Shows Early Signs of Dementia

Medicare for All Back in Play

  • Democrats took control of Congress in the midterms, helped by voter frustration over healthcare costs.
  • That shift has revived debate over Medicare for All, a key issue for current and future beneficiaries.
  • Democratic control does not guarantee Medicare for All will pass, despite hopes among some supporters.

Source: KFF Health News: Midterm Wave Sets a Course for ‘Medicare for All’ Fight
