# Quickwell ClickBank offer review

Review date: 2026-11-09

## Publishing rule

Only offers with a verified match between the seller/product and the stored affiliate destination are enabled. Public copy must distinguish seller statements from independently verified facts. HopLinks remain server-side. Affiliate disclosure appears beside recommendations. Health, safety, energy-output, water-quality, and performance claims are not repeated as established facts.

## Activations

1. Genius Switch Original — enabled first with a restrained description: “An audio-based personal development series.” No cognitive benefit, efficacy, testimonial, price, or guarantee claim is made by Quickwell.
2. Shifting Vibrations Manifest — enabled second after confirming that the exact seller page on AstralHQ matches the product name and describes the digital audio/video/PDF package. Quickwell does not repeat the seller's wealth, income, frequency, health, or manifestation outcome claims, testimonials, urgency, or guarantee language. Public copy is limited to the digital contents and explicitly states that seller claims are not verified or endorsed by Quickwell.

Both public cards link through fixed server-side redirects; HopLinks are not exposed in the catalog API.

## Re-review requirements for held offers

Before activation, confirm the exact HopLink resolves to the intended seller and product, inspect the current checkout and refund terms, match product name and label where relevant, and approve compliant, evidence-bounded public copy. Activate one offer at a time and verify its public catalog entry and redirect.
