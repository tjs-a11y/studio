Double Dose

Taking a cold medicine and a pain reliever? Many products quietly share the same active ingredient — and taking two at once can push you over a safe amount without you knowing. Type in what's on each label and this flags the overlaps. It all stays on your phone.

Information, not medical advice. This only compares the ingredient names you type from your own labels. It doesn't know your dose, your health, or how medicines interact. Read every label, and ask a pharmacist or doctor before you take or stop anything. In an emergency or a suspected overdose, call your local poison center or emergency number.
