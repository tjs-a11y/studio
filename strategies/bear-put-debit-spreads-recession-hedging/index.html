Bear Put Spreads for Downturns

Debit Spread Cyclical Downturns

A bear put spread profits from a decline with defined risk — cheaper than an outright put because the short leg offsets part of the cost — making it a common portfolio hedge into a cyclical downturn.

You expect a cyclical name or sector to fall, and want a defined-risk hedge.

Pillar 2 · When & why

Why this fits Cyclical Downturns

Used to hedge long equity exposure or express a bearish macro view. Selling the lower put trims the premium of an expensive long put during a pullback, in exchange for capping the profit at the lower strike.

Maximum loss is the debit paid, if the stock closes above the higher strike. The move has to happen before expiration, and a V-shaped bounce leaves the hedge expiring worthless.
