Pillar 2 · When & why

Why this fits Liquid ETFs

Liquid ETFs fill at pennies-wide spreads, so you lose less to friction entering, rolling and scaling across many contracts. The smoother volatility profile means fewer gap shocks than single names, at the cost of smaller premiums.

A broad fund still falls in a market sell-off, and you're assigned into it — diversification removes company risk, not market risk. Premiums are thinner than on single stocks, so returns lean on consistency and turnover, not big individual credits.
