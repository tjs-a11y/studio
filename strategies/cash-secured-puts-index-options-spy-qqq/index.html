Pillar 2 · When & why

Why this fits Range-Bound Indexes

Because an index rarely gaps the way one stock can, the focus shifts to turning the collateral over efficiently and choosing strikes with room below the range. It's an income approach for a market you expect to chop sideways, not rocket.

An index can still fall in a broad sell-off, and a short put goes in-the-money with it. The diversification removes company-specific shocks, not market risk; your collateral is committed the whole time.
