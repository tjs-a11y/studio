Pillar 2 · When & why

Why this fits Pre-Earnings Run-Ups

The method is to read the expected move from option prices, place the short strikes outside it, and often close the day after the report once the volatility crush has done its work — to avoid carrying ongoing trend risk.

Maximum loss is larger than the credit and happens if the stock gaps past a short strike. Earnings are binary; the expected move is a probability, not a fence, and a surprise can blow through a wing.
