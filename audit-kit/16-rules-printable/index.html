The 16 automatic-fail rules, on one printable page

A new US trucking company fails its FMCSA new entrant safety audit automatically if the auditor finds any one of 16 violations listed in 49 CFR 385.321(b). This sheet lists all 16 with a box to tick when you have the proof on file. Print it, pin it up, or hand it to a new driver or office manager. Free, no sign-up.

Fits on one US Letter or A4 page.

Rows 1 to 12, 14 and 15 fail on a single occurrence. If the audit fails, FMCSA sends written notice within 45 days; most carriers then have 60 days from the notice date to show corrective action (45 days for certain passenger carriers and others listed in 385.319(c)), or registration is revoked and an out-of-service order takes effect on day 61 (or 46) (385.319, 385.325). Passing these 16 is necessary, not sufficient: an audit can also fail for inadequate basic safety management controls (385.321(a)). Source: 49 CFR 385.321(b), eCFR text up to date as of Oct 8, 2026. How to use it

  1. Print one copy per company (or per audit binder).
  2. Tick a box only when you can put your hand on the proof today. If you can't find it, the auditor can't either.
  3. Rows 1 to 8 apply only if a vehicle needs a CDL. Row 10 applies only to passenger carriers. Row 1 is 382.115(a) for US carriers and 382.115(b) for foreign-domiciled carriers.
  4. For each empty box, the rule-by-rule reference explains what the regulation requires and how to fix it.
