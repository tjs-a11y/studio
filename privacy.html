Privacy

Our tools run in your browser. What you type into a tool stays on your device unless a tool clearly says otherwise.

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. When you subscribe, Stripe shares your email address, subscription status and billing country with us so we can unlock Pro and send receipts.

We keep a small token in your browser to remember that you have Pro. We don't sell personal data, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Questions or deletion requests: reply to any receipt email from Stripe, or use the "Manage subscription" link in any tool.
