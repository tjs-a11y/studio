Privacy

Our tools run in your browser. What you type into a tool stays on your device unless a tool clearly says otherwise.

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. When you buy or subscribe, Stripe shares your email address, purchase or subscription status and billing country with us so we can unlock Pro and send receipts.

Some free tools count how many times they are used. The count holds no file contents, locations or IP addresses. If you join a waitlist, we keep the email you typed only to tell you when that tool launches, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

We keep a small token in your browser to remember that you have Pro. We don't sell personal data, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Questions or deletion requests: reply to any receipt email from Stripe, or use the "Manage subscription" link in any tool.
