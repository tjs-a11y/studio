Reconcile every load, not just the total

A practical cross-check designed to make discrepancies easier to investigate.

01 · Load your records

Import mill settlement data and corresponding truck scale tickets with dates, load IDs, species, tare, and gross fields.

02 · Match deliveries

Cross-reference ticket identifiers and delivery dates, calculate net weights, and identify unmatched or inconsistent records.

03 · Inspect deductions

Flag species-code differences, weight variance, and cull or quality deductions for review against contract terms and supporting evidence.

Scale Audit is a planned arithmetic and record-matching aid, not a certified scale, timber appraisal, or legal opinion. Final determinations depend on source records, contracts, and applicable measurement standards.
