Before you sign: a small-carrier rate confirmation checklist

For owner-operators and small fleets · Updated 30 October 2026

A rate confirmation is more than the linehaul number. Read the whole document and compare it with what was agreed verbally or in writing before dispatch.

Check these items before accepting

  • Parties and load: correct broker/carrier legal names, load number, pickup and delivery locations, dates, commodity and equipment.
  • Pay: linehaul, fuel surcharge, total rate, loaded/deadhead miles and any rate-per-mile calculation you use.
  • Accessorials: detention start trigger and hourly rate, layover, TONU, lumper reimbursement, stop-offs and who must approve them.
  • Proof: arrival/departure timestamps, tracking, photos, receipts, signed POD and submission deadline.
  • Deductions: fines, chargebacks, setoff, cargo claims, service failures, re-rate language and unilateral changes.
  • Payment: payment terms, paperwork requirements, quick-pay fees and any factoring notice or assignment process.

Ask for corrections in writing

If a term is missing or unclear, request an amended rate confirmation before dispatch. Keep the original and revised copies. Do not rely on an oral promise that is absent from the final document.
