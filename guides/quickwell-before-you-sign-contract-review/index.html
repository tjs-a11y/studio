Consumer Protection

Contract Review Checklist Before Signing

Review important contract terms, fees, renewal language, cancellation rules, and obligations before you sign.

A contract or lease can look straightforward while important obligations sit in addenda, fee schedules, renewal clauses, and definitions. Translate each obligation into plain language before you commit.

A practical review process

  1. Identify the parties, service or property covered, start date, term, and conditions that must be met.
  2. Build a complete cost list: one-time charges, recurring payments, deposits, optional products, taxes, utilities, and early-exit fees.
  3. Read renewal and cancellation language. Note automatic renewal, notice windows, required delivery method, termination charges, and end-of-term obligations.
  4. Check responsibilities and remedies: maintenance, insurance, damage standards, liability limits, dispute procedures, and reporting deadlines.
  5. Ask for unclear promises or negotiated changes in the signed document or an authorized written addendum. Save the final version and proof of payment.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Relying on a verbal explanation instead of the written contract
  • Ignoring mandatory add-ons or renewal terms
  • Signing before receiving all schedules and incorporated documents

Records worth keeping

Keep the complete signed agreement, addenda, fee schedule, receipts, dated communications, and proof of cancellation or dispute notices.
