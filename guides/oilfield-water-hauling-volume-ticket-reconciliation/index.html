Oilfield water hauling: reconcile delivered volumes and meter tickets

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

Water-hauling invoices depend on a chain of records: dispatched volume, source meter, truck or trailer measurement, delivery meter and customer acceptance. Keep the units and timestamps consistent.

Capture the measurement chain

  • Job, customer, source and disposal/delivery location identifiers.
  • Start/end meter readings, meter IDs, units, timestamps and any calibration or maintenance records.
  • Truck/trailer capacity, load ticket, driver and dispatch reference.

Reconcile billed volume

  • Calculate delivered quantity from the correct start/end readings and conversion units.
  • Compare source volume, delivered volume and customer-accepted volume; document legitimate variance or loss assumptions.
  • Match the invoice quantity and rate to the service ticket and contract.
