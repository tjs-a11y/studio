Vehicle Safety

Roadside Breakdown Emergency Checklist

Follow a practical roadside breakdown checklist for visibility, personal safety, documentation, and assistance details.

A roadside breakdown can become more dangerous when people focus on the vehicle before securing the scene. Protect people first, make the vehicle visible when safe, request help, and document the event without standing in traffic.

A practical review process

  1. Move to a safe location if the vehicle can be moved without creating more danger.
  2. Turn on hazard lights and use warning devices only when you can place them without entering traffic or exposing yourself.
  3. Stay away from moving traffic. Call emergency services for injury, fire, collision, or immediate danger.
  4. Contact roadside assistance with the location, vehicle description, problem, and safe callback method.
  5. When safe, record time, location, condition, assistance provider, and details needed for insurance or repair follow-up.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Attempting repairs in an unsafe location
  • Standing behind a disabled vehicle near moving traffic
  • Sharing location or personal details publicly rather than with the assistance provider

Records worth keeping

Keep assistance case numbers, tow details, repair estimates, safe incident photos, and receipts.
