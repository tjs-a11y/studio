Before signing a carrier lease-on, equipment or factoring agreement

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

A percentage, weekly fee or advertised advance rate rarely tells the whole story. Read the full agreement and understand how money, control, liability and termination work before you commit.

Lease-on and dispatch terms

  • Commission or percentage, charge basis, settlement frequency and every recurring fee.
  • Who controls load acceptance, dispatch, fuel cards, escrow, deductions and chargebacks.
  • Insurance responsibilities, cargo claims, equipment use, maintenance and indemnity language.

Factoring and finance terms

  • Recourse or non-recourse definition, reserve/holdback, minimum volume and termination fee.
  • Notice of assignment, UCC filing, lien release process, ACH/wire fees and chargeback rules.
  • Whether the quoted rate changes by customer, invoice type or payment speed.

Equipment and rental terms

  • Who pays maintenance, tires, damage, downtime, insurance deductibles and roadside recovery.
  • Mileage limits, prohibited uses, return condition, early termination and deposit deductions.
  • Get every verbal promise and schedule incorporated into the signed document.
