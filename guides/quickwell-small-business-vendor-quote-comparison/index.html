Vendor Quote Comparison Checklist for Small Business

Compare vendor quotes by total cost, scope, exclusions, renewal terms, and service commitments before choosing a supplier.

The lowest quote is not always the lowest-cost choice. A fair comparison lines up scope, deliverables, exclusions, service levels, payment schedule, renewal terms, and the cost of changing providers.

A practical review process

  1. Write a common scope of work and send the same requirements to each vendor.
  2. Normalize quotes: setup fees, recurring charges, usage limits, taxes, minimums, and optional services over the same time horizon.
  3. Read exclusions and assumptions. Identify who supplies materials, handles revisions, covers travel, and owns work product or data.
  4. Check service and exit terms, including support hours, response commitments, renewal, cancellation, data export, and transition assistance.
  5. Document selection criteria and confirm the final scope, price, and responsibilities in the signed agreement.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Comparing quotes with different scopes
  • Ignoring renewal increases, minimum commitments, or exit costs
  • Choosing on price alone without checking capacity and service fit

Records worth keeping

Keep the request for proposal, dated quotes, comparison notes, references, final scope, contract, and change approvals.
