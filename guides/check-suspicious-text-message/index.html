How to check a suspicious text message safely

Consumer guide · Updated 31 October 2026

To check a suspicious text message safely, do not click its link or reply; verify the claim through a phone number, website or app you already know is genuine. Unexpected delivery alerts, bank warnings, toll notices, prizes and account problems are common themes scammers use to create urgency.

Use this verification checklist

  • Pause before acting: urgency, threats, unexpected fees or a prize you did not enter are reasons to slow down.
  • Do not use the message's link or phone number: open the official app yourself or type a known website address.
  • Check the sender independently: a familiar display name or caller ID is not proof the message is genuine.
  • Do not share credentials: never send a password, one-time passcode, Social Security number or full payment details in response to an unexpected text.
  • Do not reply to confirm your number: use your phone's report-junk option or your carrier's instructions instead.
  • Preserve evidence if needed: keep a screenshot or message details before reporting, without forwarding sensitive information to anyone.

Report unwanted or fraudulent texts

The Federal Trade Commission recommends forwarding unwanted text messages to 7726 (SPAM), reporting them in your messaging app, and reporting fraud at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. The FTC also advises contacting a company through a phone number or website you know is real, not the details supplied in the message.

In April 2025, the FTC reported that consumers said they lost $470 million to scams that started with text messages in 2024. The FTC's published examples include fake package-delivery messages, job offers, security alerts, unpaid-toll notices and wrong-number approaches. Those categories are warning patterns, not a way to identify the sender of any one message.

What a message checker can and cannot tell you

A text checker can highlight wording patterns and explain why a message deserves scrutiny. It cannot authenticate the sender, inspect a link's destination safely, confirm whether an account is compromised or guarantee that a message is legitimate. Verify important claims with the organization through a trusted channel.
