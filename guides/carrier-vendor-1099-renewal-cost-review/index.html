Carrier year-end vendor review: 1099 records, renewals and price increases

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

Small carriers often pay many recurring vendors: repair shops, dispatch providers, factoring services, ELD/software, parking, subscriptions and professional services. Review the records before year-end rather than reconstructing them in January.

Prepare a clean vendor export

  • Export business transactions from your bank or accounting system for the relevant tax year.
  • Normalize vendor names and separate refunds, transfers, reimbursements and personal transactions.
  • Keep invoices, W-9s, contracts and payment-method details with the accounting record.

Review the right questions

  • Which vendors may require tax reporting? Classification and exceptions matter; do not decide solely from a transaction total.
  • Which subscriptions or service contracts renewed, increased in price or added fees?
  • Which recurring charges are no longer used or duplicate another service?

Use Vendor Check carefully

  • Vendor Check can help identify payment patterns, renewal dates and price increases from an export.
  • Treat any 1099 classification as a review flag, not a tax determination. Confirm current IRS rules and consult a tax professional.
