Carrier fraud prevention: verify brokers, load offers and payment messages

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

Freight scams often rely on urgency, changed payment details or an identity that looks almost right. Verify the counterparty independently before sharing documents, dispatching a truck or changing where money is sent.

Verify the business identity

  • Check the broker's legal name, MC/USDOT authority and contact details through official FMCSA resources.
  • Call a known, independently sourced company number to confirm an unusual load, changed contact or revised payment instruction.
  • Compare the rate confirmation sender, domain, load details and payment terms with prior verified communications.

Watch for high-risk requests

  • Unexpected changes to ACH instructions, factoring remittance or payment destination.
  • Pressure to accept a load immediately, bypass normal carrier setup or share credentials and one-time codes.
  • A familiar company name paired with a slightly altered email domain, phone number or document.
