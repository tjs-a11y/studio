Timber hauling: reconcile scale tickets against mill settlements

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

When a settlement is based on weight, a small mismatch in truck tare, gross weight, ticket identity or load attribution can change the payment. Keep each trip's source tickets together.

Match each load

  • Truck/trailer identity, driver, date, trip or load number and origin/destination.
  • Gross and tare weights, net weight, scale location, ticket number and timestamp.
  • Mill receipt or delivery record that ties the ticket to the same load.

Review the settlement

  • Recalculate net weight from gross less tare and compare with the mill's accepted quantity.
  • Check deductions, moisture/quality adjustments, rejected loads, rate per ton and any minimum-weight rules.
  • Keep corrected tickets and written explanations for reweighs or adjustments.

Use Scale Audit

  • Scale Audit is designed to cross-check timber settlement statements against truck weight tickets. Verify units, ticket pairing and the mill's contract terms before treating a variance as an underpayment.
