New carrier FMCSA audit binder: records to organize before the review

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

A new carrier's first safety audit is easier to manage when records are complete, consistent and retrievable. Build the binder around the actual requirements—not a pile of unrelated PDFs.

Start with the carrier file

  • Current MCS-150 and authority details; insurance filing and BOC-3 status; company policies and responsible contacts.
  • A list of every driver who operated a commercial motor vehicle during the review period.
  • A vehicle list showing unit numbers, VINs, registration and inspection status.

Build one file per driver

  • Application and qualification documents required for the driver; license/CDL and medical qualification evidence as applicable.
  • Drug and alcohol testing program records and query/consent evidence where required.
  • Hours-of-service records, supporting documents and any applicable exemptions.

Build one file per vehicle

  • Periodic inspection and annual inspection evidence.
  • Maintenance, repair and defect reports, with dates and vehicle identifiers.
  • Proof that out-of-service defects were corrected before the vehicle returned to service.
