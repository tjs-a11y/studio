Carrier compliance mail: how to handle FMCSA, insurance and tax notices

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

A notice from FMCSA, an insurer, a state agency or a tax authority can contain a short deadline and a specific response requirement. First identify the sender and deadline; then verify the notice through an official channel.

Triage the notice

  • Record the issuing agency or company, notice date, response deadline, reference number and requested action.
  • Check the sender and case/reference number using the agency's official website or a known contact channel—not a phone number in a suspicious message.
  • Separate a real filing deadline from a payment demand or marketing solicitation.

Assign and document

  • Name the person responsible for the response and the person who will verify it before submission.
  • Save the original notice, envelope or email headers, supporting documents and proof of submission.
  • Calendar a reminder before the deadline and a follow-up to confirm receipt.

Turn the notice into a task list

  • Quickwell Pilot can extract the action, deadline, amount and contact details from a notice and draft a checklist or reply.
  • Verify every extracted date, amount, recipient and legal requirement against the original notice before acting.
