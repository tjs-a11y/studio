Carrier maintenance records: receipts, warranty dates and return windows

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

A repair receipt is more than an expense record. It may be the document needed to claim a parts warranty, return a defective component or prove when maintenance was completed.

Record every business purchase

  • Save the itemized receipt, vendor, date, amount, unit number and odometer or engine hours where relevant.
  • For tires, batteries, filters and major components, record brand, model, serial or DOT code when available.
  • Attach the repair order and note which truck or trailer received the part.

Track the deadlines

  • Record the warranty start date, duration, mileage/hour limit and exclusions from the actual warranty terms.
  • Track return windows for unused parts and core-return deadlines separately.
  • Keep proof of installation and maintenance if the warranty requires it.

Use a deadline tracker

  • Keep the receipt and warranty details in one place and review approaching deadlines before they expire.
  • Keep original records in your business file; a tracker is a reminder system, not a replacement for the vendor's warranty terms.
