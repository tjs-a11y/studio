Intermodal drayage: reconcile terminal in/out times and demurrage invoices

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

A terminal charge is only understandable when the container, appointment, gate events, free-time rules and invoice all line up. Build a single timeline for each container move.

Collect the event trail

  • Container number, booking, terminal, vessel or rail reference and dispatch/load identifiers.
  • Appointment confirmation, ingate and outgate timestamps, interchange receipts and any terminal exception notice.
  • The applicable free-time rule, holiday calendar, tariff or customer contract.

Reconcile the invoice

  • Match billed days and rate tiers to the actual gate events and the correct free-time window.
  • Separate terminal demurrage from chassis, per diem, storage, driver detention and broker/customer pass-through charges.
  • Flag missing gate events, duplicate days, incorrect container IDs and unsupported exception charges.
