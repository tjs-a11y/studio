Consumer Finance

Car Dealer Add-On Fee Checker

Review dealer add-ons, optional products, and itemized fees so you can question charges before finalizing a vehicle purchase.

Dealer add-ons can change the total amount you pay and the amount financed. Review each product and fee separately, then compare the final written contract with the itemized out-the-door quote you accepted.

Review each add-on

  1. Ask for an itemized out-the-door price in writing. Separate the vehicle price, taxes, title and registration charges, dealer fees, and optional products.
  2. For each service contract, GAP product, tire-and-wheel plan, paint protection, key replacement, or etching product, ask who provides it, what it covers, its price, exclusions, term, and cancellation rules.
  3. Ask whether each product is optional and compare equivalent coverage from the manufacturer, insurer, bank, credit union, or an independent provider.
  4. Review the financing disclosures: amount financed, APR, finance charge, total of payments, and total sale price. Optional products may increase the amount financed.
  5. Compare every page of the final contract with the written quote. Remove products you declined and keep complete copies of the signed documents.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Focusing on the monthly payment while overlooking the total amount financed and total of payments.
  • Assuming every add-on is required or every fee is unlawful; identify each item and check the written terms and applicable state rules.
  • Signing on an electronic pad before reviewing the completed contract pages.

Records worth keeping

Keep the advertised price, itemized out-the-door quote, add-on brochures, financing disclosures, product contracts, cancellation instructions, and every signed purchase document.

Store personal and financial records securely. Compare the documents you received with what you agreed to buy.
