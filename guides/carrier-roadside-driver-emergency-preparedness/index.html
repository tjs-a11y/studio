Roadside emergency readiness for drivers small fleets

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

A roadside emergency is a poor time to search for a phone number or remember how your phone's emergency feature works. Prepare a simple plan before the truck rolls.

Prepare the cab

  • Keep emergency contacts and essential medical information in a secure, accessible format, with the driver's consent.
  • Maintain required safety equipment and company emergency procedures for the vehicle and cargo.
  • Know the truck's location-sharing and dispatch check-in procedure.

Set up the phone

  • Review the phone manufacturer's real emergency SOS feature and test the setup steps without placing an emergency call.
  • Keep the phone charged and a backup charging method available.
  • Decide who should receive emergency contact notifications and keep those details current.

Make the glovebox card

  • Roadside Medical SOS Kit helps create a printable medical card and provides phone SOS setup guidance.
  • Only include information the driver chooses to disclose; protect the card from unnecessary access.
