High Utility Bill Spike Investigator

Compare utility usage and billing periods to investigate an unusually high electric, gas, or water bill.

An unusually high utility bill should be reviewed against the billing period, meter readings, rate plan, weather, and prior usage before you assume the provider made an error.

Step-by-step review

  1. Compare the billing period and number of days with prior statements.
  2. Check actual versus estimated meter readings and note any meter replacement or access issue.
  3. Compare usage units, rate plan, tier, delivery charges, taxes, and fixed fees separately.
  4. Consider seasonal conditions, occupancy changes, equipment use, leaks, or appliance faults that could affect consumption.
  5. Ask the utility for an itemized explanation and meter-reading review if the figures still do not reconcile.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Comparing totals without checking the underlying period, quantities, or work performed.
  • Relying on memory instead of dated records and original documents.
  • Assuming a checklist replaces a qualified professional or the provider's formal review process.

Records worth keeping

Keep current and prior statements, meter readings, rate-plan notices, payment records, and dated utility responses.

Keep sensitive account and identity information in an appropriate secure system. This guide is general information, not professional advice.
