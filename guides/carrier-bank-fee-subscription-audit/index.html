Carrier cash-leak audit: recurring software, bank fees and duplicate charges

Carrier operations guide · Updated 30 October 2026

A carrier's recurring overhead can hide in small monthly charges: ELD and load-board subscriptions, toll or parking services, software, bank fees and duplicate billing. Review the actual transactions before cancelling anything.

Build a review list

  • Export business checking and card transactions for a defined period.
  • Identify recurring vendors, amount changes, duplicate-looking charges and bank fees.
  • Match each charge to an active contract, service and business owner before taking action.

Separate avoidable from necessary

  • Do not cancel insurance, ELD, safety, tax or compliance services until replacement coverage and legal requirements are confirmed.
  • Check annual plans, cancellation notice periods, data-export needs and early termination fees.
  • Ask vendors for a written explanation of unexplained increases or duplicate charges.

  • Use its results as a review queue; verify each item against the statement and contract before disputing or cancelling.
