Questions

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your CSV is parsed in this page, in memory. It is never sent to a server, and — unlike a lot of "pipeline" code — no identifiers, source domains or tracking tags are ever attached to your rows. Close the tab and it's gone.

Is the spread calculator advice?

No. It computes the defined-risk math every options educator uses: spread width, maximum loss, maximum profit and breakeven. It never tells you whether to open a position, and it can't place one.

Why does it ask for the net premium?

Because a spread's maximum loss and breakeven depend on what you paid or received, not on the strikes alone. Width × 100 is only the spread's dollar width. Without a premium, this tool shows the width and tells you what else it needs — it won't print a breakeven it can't actually know.
