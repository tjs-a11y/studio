Open-Source Utility Pipeline Matrix

Normalize comma-separated data into a clean, spreadsheet-ready matrix, then explore standard defined-risk options spread arithmetic. The calculations run in this browser tab.

🔒 Local processing: The CSV you paste or select is parsed in your browser. This page does not upload the file, send its rows to an API, or save the matrix to a server. Avoid entering passwords, account numbers or other secrets.

1. Normalize a CSV

Paste CSV text or choose a .csv file. Quoted commas, escaped quotes and line breaks inside quoted fields are supported.

2. Educational spread math

Enter the sold (short) strike, protective (long) strike, contract count and optional net premium per share. Credit is positive; debit is negative.

Spread width: $5.00 per share · $1,000.00 across 2 contracts. Add a net premium to calculate maximum profit, maximum loss and breakeven.

Privacy and limitations

  • CSV parsing and calculations execute locally in this browser page. No CSV content is sent to Quickwell.
  • The page does not request bank or brokerage credentials and does not execute trades or hold funds.
  • Downloaded CSV files are created locally by your browser. Your browser's download settings determine where they are saved.
  • Options results are simplified arithmetic for educational purposes. Fees, taxes, early assignment, liquidity, exercise and market conditions are not modeled.

Educational disclaimer: Nothing here is financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and no output is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold a security or options position.
